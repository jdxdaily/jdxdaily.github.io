US Stocks Rise as Tech Sector Leads with +0.60% Gain in Nasdaq 100

The US stock market ended the previous session on a positive note, with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 rising by +0.70% and +0.60% respectively. However, beneath the surface, there are concerns about the Russell 2000 which fell by -0.50%, indicating potential weakness in the broader market.
