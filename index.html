US Markets End Mixed as Energy and Industrials Lead Gains

The US market ended the previous session with mixed results, as the S&P 500 and Dow Jones declined by 0.17% and 0.20% respectively, while the Nasdaq 100 dropped 0.13%. However, the Russell 2000 bucked the trend, rising 0.51%. Below the surface, sector rotation was notable, with energy and industrials leading the gains.
