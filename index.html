US equities rally on tech earnings, while energy drags

The S&P 500 closed at 7,722.60, up +0.60%, driven by a second straight week of strong tech earnings. NVDA and AVGO posted double‑digit gains, reinforcing the Nasdaq 100’s +1.29% advance. Meanwhile, the energy sector fell sharply as XLE slipped -2.05% on a 1.73% rise in WTI, suggesting demand concerns outweigh price gains.
