TL;DR & Editorial Take

The US market saw a rebound in the Dow Jones, rising +0.51% to 52,210.08, while the Nasdaq 100 declined -0.32% to 28,039.21. The +0.51% gain in the Dow Jones is a positive sign, but the -0.32% decline in the Nasdaq 100 is a cause for concern. The market is waiting for the next catalyst, which could come from the upcoming earnings season or the Fed's decision on interest rates. The technical picture is mixed, with the SPY trading above its 50-day moving average but below its 200-day moving average. The QQQ is trading below its 50-day and 200-day moving averages, indicating a bearish trend. The IWM is trading above its 50-day moving average but below its 200-day moving average, indicating a neutral trend.
