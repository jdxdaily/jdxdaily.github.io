US equities rally on tech strength, yields edge higher

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,743.41, up +0.51%, driven by solid earnings from Microsoft and Nvidia and a retreat in the VIX to 14.87. The 10‑year Treasury yield rose to 5.18, reflecting continued confidence in the Fed’s restrictive stance.

Energy lagged as WTI fell -2.33%, while the dollar index slipped -0.32%. Investors should watch upcoming earnings from Apple and Amazon for directional cues.
