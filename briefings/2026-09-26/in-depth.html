US equities rally on tech earnings, while rate outlook tightens

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,743.41, up +0.51%, driven by a broad tech bounce and resilient consumer‑finance sentiment. 10‑year yields rose to 5.18%, reinforcing expectations of a single 25‑bp Fed cut later this year. Volatility receded, with the VIX at 14.87 (-5.11%).

While the rally appears solid, the market remains vulnerable to three headwinds: (1) a surprise acceleration in core inflation, (2) renewed geopolitical tension in the Middle East, and (3) a potential pull‑back in AI‑related valuations after the latest earnings season.

Our stance: maintain a core‑plus bias toward high‑quality growth with selective defensive overlay. Watch for sector rotation into financials and industrials if rate‑sensitivity intensifies.

  • Broad market breadth: 9 of 11 sector ETFs in the green; XLE and XLC the only losers.
  • Key earnings: MSFT (+3.66%) and NVDA (+0.22%) set the tone.
  • Rate outlook: 10‑year yield at 5.18%, markets pricing a 25‑bp cut with ~30% probability.
