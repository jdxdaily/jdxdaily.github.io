US equities rally on tech earnings, yields slip

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,743.41, up +0.51%, driven by a second‑day surge in mega‑cap tech after strong earnings from MSFT and NVDA. The rally coincided with a modest decline in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 5.18% and a 5% pull‑back in the VIX, signalling reduced near‑term risk appetite.

Behind the headline numbers, the market is re‑pricing a softer inflation outlook and a narrowing yield curve, while the energy sector remains under pressure as WTI slipped below 92 USD. Investors should watch for any surprise in upcoming CPI data and the Fed’s minutes for clues on the pace of future rate cuts.
