US Stocks Rise as Tech Leads the Charge, NVDA and META Soar

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The US market saw a broad-based rally, with the S&P 500 rising +0.65% and the Nasdaq 100 gaining +1.15%. The move was led by the tech sector, with NVDA and META being the top performers. Below the surface, the 10-year yield fell -0.88%, while WTI crude dropped -2.31%.

Looking ahead, the market will be watching the US data calendar, which includes the release of the Empire State Manufacturing Survey and the NAHB Housing Market Index. The earnings calendar is also heating up, with several major companies set to report this week. In geopolitics, tensions between the US and China remain a concern, with the ongoing trade negotiations and the situation in the South China Sea being closely watched.
