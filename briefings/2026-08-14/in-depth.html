TL;DR & Editorial Take

The US stock market extended its gains on Wednesday, with the SPY closing up +0.65% and the QQQ up +1.15%. The rally was led by the tech sector, with NVDA and META among the top performers. Our editorial take is that the market is due for a pullback, given the recent run-up and the lack of significant catalysts on the horizon.

The key drivers of the market's rally have been the strong earnings season and the continued decline in interest rates. However, with the 10Y yield now at 4.64, we believe that the easy money has already been made, and investors should be cautious of a potential reversal.

Our top three takeaways from the day's action are:

  • The tech sector is leading the charge, with NVDA and META among the top performers.
  • The market is due for a pullback, given the recent run-up and the lack of significant catalysts on the horizon.
  • Investors should be cautious of a potential reversal, given the decline in interest rates and the current valuation levels.
