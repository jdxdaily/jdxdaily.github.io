US equities rally on strong earnings and easing inflation expectations

The full picture: macro, technicals, options, analyst moves, sector internals, international context, and a full week ahead.

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,743.41, up +0.51%, as earnings beat expectations across technology and financials while the VIX fell to 14.87, a 5.1% decline. The 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.18%, reinforcing the view that the Fed’s tightening cycle is nearing its end.

Macro data showed a modest rise in WTI crude to 94.14 USD and a 0.17% gain in the dollar index, but gold retreated to 4,211.90 USD, underscoring a risk‑on bias. Crypto assets slipped, with BTC at 83,184.78 USD.

Our editorial stance: maintain a core‑plus equity bias, favouring high‑quality growth names that have demonstrated pricing power, while trimming exposure to lagging consumer discretionary and energy stocks.

  • Core equity exposure remains overweight S&P 500, underweight Energy.
  • Increase allocation to defensive health‑care and financials on the back of earnings strength.
  • Maintain a modest hedge using short‑dated VIX calls and Treasury put spreads.
