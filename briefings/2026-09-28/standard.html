US equities rally on tech earnings, while yields climb

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed above 7,700 for the first time this month, driven by a second‑day surge in mega‑cap tech after MSFT and NVDA posted better‑than‑expected earnings. The rally lifted the Nasdaq 100 and Dow, but the VIX slipped below 15, signalling waning fear.

Behind the headline, the 10‑year Treasury yield rose to 5.18 %, tightening financing conditions for growth stocks. At the same time, energy prices jumped on OPEC‑plus output cuts, keeping the XLE sector in the red. Investors should watch the yield curve for signs of a flattening that could pressure high‑beta names.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Unexpected acceleration in core CPI above 2.5% could push yields past 5.5%.
  • Geopolitical escalation in the Middle East raising oil prices above $100 bbl.
  • Weakening of the dollar (DXY < 100) eroding commodity price gains.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • Further AI‑related earnings beats from NVDA or GOOGL.
  • Positive surprise in ISM services index (above 53).
  • Eurozone inflation cooling below 2% prompting dovish ECB tone.

Positioning notes

  • Yield‑sensitive sectors (real‑estate, utilities) face headwinds; consider defensive quality.
  • AI‑driven tech remains the primary upside engine; allocate selectively to chip and cloud names.
  • Energy price rally offers short‑term upside for XLE but watch for profit‑taking on higher oil.
