US equities rally on strong earnings, yields inch higher

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

All three major indices closed higher on Friday, with the S&P 500 up +0.51% to 7,743.41 and the Dow gaining +0.93% to 51,828.62. The rally was anchored by solid earnings from MSFT and NVDA, while the 10‑year Treasury yield rose to 5.18%.

Volatility eased as the VIX fell -5.11% to 14.87, and the dollar index nudged higher. Energy prices jumped, with WTI crude up +1.87% to 94.14 USD.
