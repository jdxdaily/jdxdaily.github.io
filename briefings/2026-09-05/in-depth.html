US equities slip as rates rise, tech shows resilience

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,718.60, down -0.38%, while the Nasdaq 100 edged higher at +0.21%. Higher‑than‑expected 10‑year Treasury yields (4.78%, +0.46%) pressured rate‑sensitive sectors, but the tech‑heavy XLK rallied +0.70% on strong earnings outlook for NVDA and META. Breadth remains thin; only 4 of 11 sector ETFs posted gains, underscoring a risk‑off tone.

Our editorial view: the market is entering a consolidation phase. With the Fed likely to hold rates steady through Q4, investors will price in a modest upside to equities if inflation data stay subdued. However, any surprise in core CPI or a sharper rise in the 10‑year yield could trigger a sell‑off, especially in financials and consumer discretionary.

Key take‑aways for portfolio construction:

  • Maintain exposure to high‑quality growth names (NVDA, META) that benefit from AI and digital ad spend.
  • Reduce weight in rate‑sensitive sectors (XLF, XLY) until the yield curve stabilises.
  • Consider short‑duration Treasury or cash overlays as a hedge against further yield spikes.
