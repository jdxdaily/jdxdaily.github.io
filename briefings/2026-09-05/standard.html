US equities slip as tech drags, yields rise

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 fell -0.38% to 7,718.60 while the Nasdaq 100 eked out a modest gain +0.21%. The decline was led by heavyweight tech names, notably AAPL and MSFT, which posted double‑digit percentage drops. Meanwhile, the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped +0.46% to 4.78%, pressuring growth valuations.

Behind the numbers, the market is digesting a mixed macro backdrop: higher yields, a resilient VIX at 14.53, and a modest rebound in energy prices. The combination of rising rates and softening tech earnings creates a bearish bias for the next session, though defensive sectors such as utilities and consumer staples showed relative strength.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Further upside in the 10‑year yield above 5.00%.
  • Escalation of geopolitical risk in the Middle East.
  • Disappointing Q4 earnings from AAPL or MSFT.
  • Unexpected drop in US consumer confidence.
BULLISH TRIGGERS

  • Resolution of crypto regulatory concerns boosting BTC.
  • Fed signals a pause or cut in policy rates.
