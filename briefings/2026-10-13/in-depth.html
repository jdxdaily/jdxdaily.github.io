US equities slip as tech drags, while commodities rally on weaker dollar

The full picture: macro, technicals, options, analyst moves, sector internals, international context, and a full week ahead.

20-minute read · Topics: All of the above, plus technicals, options, ratings, week-ahead Download PDF

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed flat at 7,807.24, the Nasdaq 100 fell -0.71% and the Dow edged up +0.38%. Broad market breadth remains negative – only 4 of 11 sector ETFs posted gains and the VIX jumped +2.49% to 15.21, signalling rising risk aversion.

Energy and commodities benefited from a softer dollar and a modest pull‑back in oil prices, while the tech sector remains the drag, led by NVDA and AMZN posting double‑digit declines. The 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.24%, keeping financing costs elevated.

Our view: investors should trim exposure to high‑beta tech, favour defensive sectors (healthcare, consumer staples) and consider short‑dated volatility hedges as the market tests the 7,800 S&P level.

  • Maintain core equity exposure at 70‑75% with a bias toward XLV and XLP ETFs.
  • Allocate 5‑7% to short‑dated VIX calls or puts depending on risk appetite.
  • Watch the 10‑year yield for a break below 5.20% as a potential catalyst for a broader rally.
