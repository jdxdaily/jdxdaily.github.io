US equities slip as tech drags, while energy and commodities rally

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 hovered at 7,807.24, barely down -0.06%, as the Nasdaq‑100 led the decline with a -0.71% slide. Broad‑based weakness stemmed from a second‑half tech pull‑back, highlighted by NVDA and AMZN falling more than 1% each.

Meanwhile, energy‑focused ETFs outperformed on a +1.43% jump in WTI crude, and the VIX rose to 15.21 (+2.49%), signalling rising risk aversion. The 10‑year Treasury slipped to 5.24%, keeping financing costs modest for the upcoming earnings season.
