TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,718.60, down -0.38%, as earnings disappointment from AAPL and MSFT dragged the index lower. The Nasdaq 100 bucked the trend, edging up +0.21% on strength in AI‑related names, while the Dow fell -0.51% on financials and industrials.

Bond yields rose, with the 10‑year Treasury at 4.78% (+0.46%), reinforcing a modestly tighter monetary outlook. The VIX climbed to 14.53 (+1.47%), reflecting heightened near‑term volatility expectations.

Our view: the market is entering a short‑term risk‑off phase. Defensive positioning is prudent until the earnings cycle clears and the Fed signals a clearer path on rates.

  • Maintain core equity exposure, but tilt toward defensive sectors (Utilities, Consumer Staples).
  • Consider short‑duration bond exposure to hedge rising yields.
  • Watch for a breakout in AI‑related stocks as earnings momentum re‑ignites.
