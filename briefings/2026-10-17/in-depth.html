TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 slipped -1.09% to 7,659.44, driven by a broad tech sell‑off and a 10‑year Treasury yield jump to 5.10%. The VIX surged +21.45% to 19.99, underscoring heightened volatility expectations.

Rate‑sensitive sectors under pressure while energy rallied on higher crude. Defensive financials posted the only sector‑wide gain, reflecting a flight‑to‑quality amid tightening monetary conditions.

Our editorial view: the market is entering a risk‑off phase. Expect further downside in growth stocks until the Fed signals a pause or a clear path to rate cuts.

  • Broad market downtrend confirmed by all major indices.
  • 10‑year yield at 5.10% – highest since 2023, tightening financing conditions.
  • VIX at 19.99 – a warning flag for volatility‑sensitive portfolios.
