US equities tumble as yields rise and volatility spikes

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 slipped -1.09% to 7,659.44, while the Nasdaq 100 fell -2.50% to 29,187.55. The VIX surged +21.45% to 19.99, reflecting heightened risk appetite after the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped to 5.10%.

Energy led the rally with XLE up +2.52% as WTI crude breached 101.41. Financials were the only sector in the green, with XLF gaining +0.45% on a stronger JPM.
