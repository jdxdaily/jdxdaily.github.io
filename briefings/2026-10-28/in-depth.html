TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,722.60, up +0.60%, driven by a +1.70% jump in the XLK technology ETF and a +4.04% surge in TSLA. Core inflation remains sticky, keeping the 10‑year Treasury yield at 4.99% and the VIX at a low 17.52. Market breadth is positive but narrowing, with 7 of 11 sectors in the green.

Our view: the rally is justified on earnings momentum, yet the risk of a higher‑than‑expected CPI print could trigger a rapid unwind. Defensive positioning is advisable ahead of the Fed’s November meeting.

Key take‑aways:

  • Tech and consumer discretionary lead; energy remains the weakest sector.
  • Yield curve steepening continues – 10‑year at 4.99%, 2‑year at 5.12%.
  • Options flow shows a net +12% bias toward calls on the S&P 500, but put volume spikes on XLE.
