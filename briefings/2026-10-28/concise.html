A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

The Nasdaq 100 surged +1.29% to 29,651.44, pulling the broader market higher as chip and cloud stocks posted solid gains. The S&P 500 rose +0.60% to 7,722.60, while the VIX fell -4.73% to 17.52, signaling reduced volatility.

Yield pressures remain modest with the 10‑year Treasury at 4.99% (+0.14%). Energy lagged as XLE dropped -2.05% on a 101.63 USD WTI price. Investors eye upcoming earnings from AAPL and MSFT later this week.
