US equities rally on tech earnings, while energy drags

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,722.60, up +0.60%, driven by a second straight week of strong tech earnings. NVDA and AVGO posted double‑digit gains, reinforcing the Nasdaq 100’s +1.29% advance. Meanwhile, the energy sector fell sharply as XLE slipped -2.05% on a 1.73% rise in WTI, suggesting demand concerns outweigh price gains.

Behind the headline numbers, the VIX retreated to 17.52 (-4.73%), indicating waning volatility risk. The 10‑year Treasury yield nudged to 4.99% (+0.14%), keeping the yield curve steep and supporting rate‑sensitive growth stocks. Investors should watch the Fed’s upcoming policy minutes for clues on the trajectory of rates.

Spotlight: tech earnings surge fuels Nasdaq rally

The Nasdaq 100’s +1.29% gain was anchored by a cluster of earnings beats from AI‑centric names. NVDA posted a 0.90% rise after reporting Q3 revenue of 13.2 billion, 18% above consensus, driven by data‑center GPU demand. AVGO surged 4.00% on a 4.5% revenue beat and a new 5G chipset contract worth 1.1 billion. TSLA outperformed with a 4.04% jump after announcing record deliveries and a marginal price‑cut impact.
