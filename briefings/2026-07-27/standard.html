US Stocks End Mixed as Investors Weigh Earnings and Economic Data

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The US stock market ended the previous session on a mixed note, with the Dow Jones rising +0.46% and the Nasdaq 100 falling -1.15%. Below the surface, sector rotation was a key theme, with investors favoring defensive sectors such as consumer staples and healthcare. The VIX declined -0.64% to 18.58, indicating a decrease in market volatility.

The market's focus is shifting towards the upcoming earnings season, with several major companies set to report their quarterly results. Investors are also keeping a close eye on economic data, including the latest inflation numbers and job market reports. The 10Y Yield fell -0.51% to 4.68, while WTI Crude dropped -4.96% to 84.88.
