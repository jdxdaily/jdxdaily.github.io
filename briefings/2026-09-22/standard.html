US equities rally on tech earnings, meta surge fuels optimism

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 jumped +1.49% to 7,764.70, driven by a strong tech earnings beat and a surprise rebound in META, which surged +11.43%. The rally lifted risk assets across the board, while the VIX slipped to its lowest level of the year at 14.87, signaling reduced near‑term volatility.

Behind the headline numbers, the market is pricing in a softer inflation outlook after the 10‑year Treasury slipped to 4.96% and crude oil retreated below 94. However, the upside is vulnerable to any surprise in upcoming CPI data and the Fed’s next policy decision, which could reignite rate‑sensitivity in growth stocks.

Spotlight: meta platforms earnings

Meta posted Q3 revenue of 38.2 billion, up 12% YoY, and EPS of 9.84, beating consensus of 9.20. The headline driver was a 22% jump in ad impressions, helped by AI‑enhanced targeting.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • US CPI coming in above 0.4% could reignite rate‑hike expectations.
  • Unexpected drop in tech earnings guidance, especially from NVDA or MSFT.
  • Geopolitical escalation in the Middle East that spikes oil above 100 USD/barrel.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • Core PCE below 0.2% reinforcing a rate‑cut outlook.
  • Meta’s Q4 guidance beating expectations, lifting ad‑spend sentiment.
  • Strong Chinese manufacturing PMI, supporting global demand.
