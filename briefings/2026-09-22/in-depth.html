TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 surged +1.49% to 7,764.70, powered by a blockbuster earnings beat from META and continued strength in the AI‑heavy Nasdaq. Defensive sectors lagged, with energy down -2.30% on a sharp WTI slide. Crypto’s resurgence, led by Bitcoin’s +5.42% rally, adds a speculative tailwind for risk‑on sentiment.

Liquidity remains ample as the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 4.96% (-0.70%). The VIX stayed low at 14.87, indicating muted volatility expectations. However, the modest rise in the DXY and a still‑elevated inflation backdrop keep the Fed’s policy outlook uncertain.

Our stance: maintain a core long bias on high‑growth tech, trim exposure to lagging energy, and consider selective crypto‑linked hedges for tail‑risk.

  • Long: NVDA, META, MSFT
  • Short/underweight: XLE (energy), XLP (consumer staples)
  • Hedge: 0‑DTE put spreads on SPY and Bitcoin futures
