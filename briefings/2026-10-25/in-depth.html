TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,623.68, down -0.52%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped to 4.99% (+0.71%). Energy rallied on a +3.82% surge in WTI, while technology and financials lagged.

Higher‑for‑longer rates are pressuring growth stocks, and the VIX’s rise to 19.45 signals renewed volatility. Breadth remains thin; only 4 of 11 sector ETFs posted gains.

We remain cautious ahead of the Fed’s November meeting. Expect continued defensive positioning and selective long‑bias in energy and commodities.

  • Yield curve steepening: 10Y at 4.99% versus 2Y near 4.75%.
  • Energy outperformance: XLE up +2.60% on oil rally.
  • Tech weakness: XLK down -0.49%, NVDA off -0.59%.
