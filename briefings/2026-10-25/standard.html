US equities slip as yields rise and oil spikes

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,623.68, down -0.52%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped to 4.99% (+0.71%) and WTI crude surged past 103 USD. The market’s downside bias reflects renewed inflation pressure from higher energy prices and a flattening yield curve that keeps the Fed’s next rate move uncertain.

Sector breadth was thin; defensive financials (XLF) and consumer staples (XLP) led declines, while energy (XLE) posted the only sector gain. The Nasdaq’s flat performance masks a pull‑back in mega‑cap tech, with NVDA slipping -0.59% after a weaker‑than‑expected guidance update.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Further escalation in Middle‑East supply disruptions pushing oil above 110 USD.
  • Unexpected CPI print > 0.3 % MoM, reinforcing “higher‑for‑longer” rates.
  • Sharp sell‑off in tech earnings, especially if NVDA cuts guidance.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • Oil price pull‑back below 95 USD, relieving inflation pressure.
  • Positive Fed commentary on a possible rate pause.
  • Strong earnings surprises from MSFT or AAPL that lift sentiment.

Positioning notes

  • Weight toward energy and rate‑sensitive financials as yields stay elevated.
  • Maintain defensive exposure in consumer staples and utilities to hedge volatility.
  • Monitor tech valuation compression; selective long positions only on clear earnings catalysts.
