US equities slip as yields rise, tech leads modest rally

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 edged lower -0.25% while the Nasdaq 100 posted a modest gain +0.23%. The market’s split reflects a tug‑of‑war between higher 10‑year yields, now at 5.29% (+0.72%), and resilient demand for growth‑oriented tech stocks.

Behind the headline numbers, the VIX climbed to 16.34 (+1.87%), signaling rising volatility expectations. Sector breadth remains thin; defensive financials and industrials lag, while the technology sector, anchored by NVDA and MSFT, is the only source of net buying.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • 10‑year yield breaches 5.40% – further equity pressure.
  • Fed hints at a second rate hike in November.
  • Unexpected rise in CPI above 0.4% MoM.
  • Geopolitical escalation in the Middle East disrupting oil supply.
BULLISH TRIGGERS

  • VIX drops below 15.0, indicating risk appetite.
  • Eurozone inflation eases, prompting dovish ECB tone.
