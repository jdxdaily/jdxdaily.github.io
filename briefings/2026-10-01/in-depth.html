US equities slip as yields rise, tech shows resilience

The full picture: macro, technicals, options, analyst moves, sector internals, international context, and a full week ahead.

20-minute read · Topics: All of the above, plus technicals, options, ratings, week-ahead Download PDF

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,651.54, down 0.25%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped +0.72% to 5.29%. Higher financing costs pressured financials and industrials, while the Nasdaq 100 held firm with a +0.23% gain, driven by continued strength in NVDA and MSFT. The market remains in a risk‑off mode, reflected by a VIX rise to 16.34 (+1.87%).

Our editorial view: the upside for equities is now contingent on a clear signal that the Fed will pause rate hikes. Without that, the breadth remains thin and defensive sectors are likely to underperform. Investors should prioritize quality, cash‑flow generation and hedge against further yield spikes.

Key take‑aways:

  • Yield curve steepening adds pressure to rate‑sensitive stocks; monitor 10‑year yield above 5.30% as a bearish trigger.
  • Tech fundamentals remain robust; expect continued outperformance from AI‑driven names.
  • Protective positioning via VIX futures or long‑dated put spreads is advisable given the rising volatility index.
