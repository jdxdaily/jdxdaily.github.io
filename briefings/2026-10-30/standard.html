US equities slip as rate anxiety outweighs tech rebound

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,720.39, down -0.30%, while the Nasdaq 100 fell -1.38% on heightened sensitivity to the 10‑year Treasury yield, which slipped to 4.99% after a modest pull‑back in inflation expectations. Despite a rally in the Russell 2000 and defensive sector ETFs, broad‑based risk appetite remains muted.

Behind the headline numbers, the market is digesting mixed corporate earnings and a still‑elevated VIX at 17.20. The dollar’s slight decline (-0.21%) offers a modest tailwind to commodities, yet crypto’s sharp drop (-3.68% for BTC) signals continued stress in risk‑off assets.
