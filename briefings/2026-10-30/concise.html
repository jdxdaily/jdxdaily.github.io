US equities slip as earnings drag, yields retreat

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

The major indices closed lower on Friday, with the S&P 500 down 0.30% at 7,720.39 and the Nasdaq 100 off 1.38% at 29,402.51. Earnings pressure from AMZN and a 10‑year Treasury yield pull‑back to 4.99% weighed on risk assets.

Defensive sectors rallied, led by financials (+1.44%) and health care (+2.40%). The VIX rose to 17.20, signalling heightened volatility.
