US equities rally on tech earnings, yields ease

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,722.72, up +0.73%, as strong earnings from NVDA and AVGO lifted the tech and semiconductor sub‑indexes. The 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.28, supporting risk assets while the VIX fell to its lowest level since June.

Behind the headline gains, the market is pricing in a softer Fed stance after the latest FOMC minutes and a modest dip in oil. However, the upside is capped by lingering concerns over China’s property sector and a still‑elevated inflation backdrop.

Macro & Fed

FOMC minutes show most participants expect one more 25‑bp hike before year‑end, but inflation trajectory is “moderately easing.” The 10‑year yield’s slight rise to 5.28 reflects that nuance. Yield curve remains mildly inverted (2‑year at 5.45%, 10‑year at 5.28%).
