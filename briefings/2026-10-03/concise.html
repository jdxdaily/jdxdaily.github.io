TL;DR

The major indices closed higher on Monday, with the Nasdaq 100 up +1.00% and the S&P 500 up +0.73%. Broad‑based buying was led by technology and consumer discretionary ETFs, while the 10‑year Treasury yield fell to 5.28%.

Key catalysts were better‑than‑expected earnings from NVDA and AVGO, and a softer CPI print that kept inflation expectations in check. The VIX dropped to 15.31, indicating reduced market fear.
