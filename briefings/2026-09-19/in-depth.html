US equities edge higher as tech rebounds, but rate risk lingers

The full picture: macro, technicals, options, analyst moves, sector internals, international context, and a full week ahead.

20-minute read · Topics: All of the above, plus technicals, options, ratings, week-ahead Download PDF

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 nudged up +0.17% to 7,650.50 while the Nasdaq 100 surged +0.67%, driven by a rebound in semiconductor stocks and a firmer NVDA. 10‑year Treasury yields slipped back to 5.00% after the Fed’s “wait‑and‑see” stance, keeping the VIX at a low‑risk 14.81. Crypto rallied sharply, with Bitcoin up +6.05%, reflecting renewed appetite for risk assets.

However, the Dow Jones slipped -0.18%, and defensive sectors remain under pressure, signalling that any surprise in upcoming CPI or Fed minutes could reignite volatility. Investors should watch the 10‑year yield’s 200‑day moving average (~5.03%) for a cue on rate‑sensitivity.

Our stance: maintain a core equity exposure tilted toward growth‑oriented tech, but hedge with short‑dated Treasury puts or VIX futures to guard against a sudden rate‑hike surprise.

  • Tech‑heavy ETFs (XLK, QQQ) lead the rally; defensive ETFs (XLP, XLU) lag.
  • 10‑year yield at 5.00% – still above the 2‑year/10‑year spread peak of 0.45%.
  • Crypto’s +6‑7% weekly bounce adds a non‑correlated risk‑on tail.
