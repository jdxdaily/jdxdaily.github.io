US equities edge higher as tech leads, yields climb

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 nudged up +0.17% to 7,650.50, driven by a solid tech rebound while the Dow slipped -0.18%. Behind the headline, the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped +1.03% to 5.00%, tightening financing conditions for rate‑sensitive sectors.

Crypto rallied sharply, with Bitcoin up +6.05% and Ether up +7.36%, reflecting renewed risk appetite after the Fed’s dovish tone. The VIX fell to 14.81, the lowest level in three weeks, underscoring a market that is pricing in a lower probability of near‑term volatility spikes.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Unexpected acceleration in core CPI could push yields above 5.20%, hurting rate‑sensitive sectors.
  • Geopolitical escalation in the Middle East raising oil prices above $110 per barrel.
  • Weak Q4 earnings from major banks, especially if loan loss provisions rise.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • Further AI‑chip revenue beats, especially from NVDA and AMD, sustaining tech momentum.
  • Positive surprise in existing‑home sales indicating resilient consumer spending.
  • Resolution of Middle‑East tensions, removing the oil‑supply premium.

Positioning notes

  • Allocate modest exposure to AI‑centric tech while keeping cash for potential yield‑driven pullbacks.
  • Maintain defensive tilt in utilities and real estate as yields remain elevated.
  • Consider opportunistic long positions in commodities‑linked equities if oil stabilises below $105.
