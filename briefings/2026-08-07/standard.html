US Stocks Slip as Yields Rise, Oil Surges

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The US stock market experienced a decline in the previous session, with the S&P 500 falling by 0.18% to 7,709.96, and the Nasdaq 100 dropping by 0.39% to 29,373.33. This downturn was largely driven by rising yields, with the 10Y Yield increasing by +1.15% to 4.67, and a surge in oil prices, as WTI Crude rose by +3.68% to 77.99.

Below the surface, the rotation out of growth and into value continued, with MSFT gaining +2.54% and GOOGL falling by -1.29%. The VIX also decreased by -4.17% to 15.15, indicating a decrease in market volatility.
