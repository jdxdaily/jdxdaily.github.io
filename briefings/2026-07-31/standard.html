US Stocks Surge as Tech Leads the Charge

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The US market saw a significant surge yesterday, with the Nasdaq 100 leading the charge with a +3.36% gain. The rally was driven by a strong performance from tech stocks, with MSFT and NVDA being among the top gainers. However, beneath the surface, there are concerns about the sustainability of this rally, given the ongoing macroeconomic uncertainties and the potential for a pullback in the near term.

A closer look at the sector rotation reveals that the XLK sector ETF was the biggest gainer, up +5.50%, while the XLC and XLP sector ETFs were among the laggards, down -2.68% and -2.16% respectively. This rotation suggests that investors are becoming increasingly risk-on, but it also highlights the potential for a correction in the tech sector.

Spotlight

The biggest event of the day was the earnings report from MSFT, which saw the stock surge +15.51%. The report highlighted strong growth in the company's cloud business, with Azure revenue up +40% year-over-year. The report also provided guidance for the next quarter, which was above consensus estimates.
