TL;DR

The US market saw a significant rally in the last session, led by the tech sector, with the Nasdaq 100 closing +3.36% higher at 28,106.35. This surge was largely driven by strong performances from large-cap tech stocks, including MSFT which gained +15.51% and NVDA with a +2.65% increase.

Today, investors will be watching for further developments in the tech sector, as well as macroeconomic indicators and geopolitical events that could impact the market. The Dow Jones and S&P 500 also saw gains, closing +1.19% and +1.66% higher, respectively.
