US equities tumble as tech leads broad sell‑off, volatility spikes

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 slipped -1.82% to 7,580.07, dragging the Nasdaq 100 down -3.46% amid a sharp correction in mega‑cap chips. NVDA led the decline with a -4.22% slide, pulling the XLK semiconductor ETF lower -4.89%. The VIX surged +12.91% to 19.42, underscoring heightened risk appetite.

Behind the numbers, the market reacted to weaker-than‑expected earnings guidance from AVGO and fresh concerns over the Fed’s “higher‑for‑longer” stance after the latest FOMC minutes. Energy rallied on a +2.08% jump in WTI, while defensive sectors (XLP, XLV, XLU) posted modest gains, hinting at a short‑term flight‑to‑quality.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Further downgrade of chip earnings guidance (e.g., NVDA or AVGO).
  • Unexpected rise in CPI above 0.4% MoM, prompting tighter monetary stance.
  • Escalation of Middle East conflict disrupting oil supply.
BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Surprise upside in US retail sales, reinforcing consumer resilience.
  • Strong earnings beat from a defensive heavyweight (e.g., KO or UNH).
  • Resolution of EU‑US trade talks, easing geopolitical risk.

Positioning notes

  • Risk‑off bias is evident; overweight defensive ETFs (XLP, XLV, XLU) and underweight cyclical exposure.
  • Yield curve flattening suggests caution on duration; short‑duration bonds may outperform.
  • Tech valuation compression creates potential entry points if earnings guidance improves.
