US equities rally on tech earnings, yields rise as Fed signals tighter policy

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,773.95, up +0.66%, driven by a second‑day surge in mega‑cap tech after strong earnings from NVDA and MSFT. 10‑year Treasury yields jumped to 5.31% (+0.64%), reflecting renewed expectations of a second Fed rate hike in Q4. Commodity markets were mixed: WTI slipped -1.06% to 88.48, while gold held near 4,158.60 amid a modest rise in the DXY.

Our editorial view: the market’s upside remains contingent on tech earnings staying ahead of expectations and the Fed’s policy stance staying transparent. Any surprise downgrade in guidance or a sharper‑than‑expected inflation print could trigger a rapid swing in sentiment.

Key take‑aways for investors:

  • Tech earnings momentum supports the rally, but valuation pressure is building on NVDA and TSLA.
  • Rising yields increase cost of capital for growth stocks; monitor the 10‑year threshold at 5.35%.
  • Energy sector outperforms on price gains; consider overweighting XLE if oil stays above 90.
