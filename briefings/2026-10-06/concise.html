US equities rally on earnings strength, yields inch higher

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

The major indices closed sharply higher on Thursday, with the S&P 500 up +0.66% to 7,773.95 and the Nasdaq 100 gaining +0.87% to 31,076.44. Broad‑based buying was led by technology and financials, while the VIX rose modestly, reflecting lingering volatility.

Yield pressures persisted as the 10‑year Treasury yield climbed to 5.31% (+0.64%). Oil slipped below $90, and crypto assets retreated, but gold held steady above $4,150.
