US equities rally on tech earnings, yields climb

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,773.95, up +0.66%, driven by a broad tech bounce after NVDA and MSFT posted better‑than‑expected earnings. The rally lifted the Nasdaq 100 to a fresh 31‑year high, while the VIX rose modestly, signalling lingering volatility ahead of the Fed’s next policy decision.

Behind the headline numbers, the market is digesting a tightening yield curve—10‑year Treasury yields jumped +0.64% to 5.31%—and a modest pullback in oil at 88.48 USD. Energy ETFs led sector gains, but defensive health‑care and utilities remain resilient, hinting at a cautious rotation toward quality ahead of the upcoming FOMC minutes.
