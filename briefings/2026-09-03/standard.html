US equities rally on strong earnings and easing volatility

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,666.60, up +0.46%, as earnings beat expectations across technology and consumer discretionary, while the VIX fell to 15.20, a ‑6.98% drop, signalling reduced risk appetite. Gold surged +2.48% to 4,474.80, reflecting safe‑haven demand amid lingering geopolitical tension in the Middle East.

Behind the headline numbers, NVDA posted a +3.21% jump on its AI‑chip roadmap, and the financial sector outperformed on higher‑for‑longer rate expectations. The market’s upside is capped by a flat 10‑year yield at 4.80% and a modest dip in the dollar index, leaving room for a pull‑back if macro data disappoint.
