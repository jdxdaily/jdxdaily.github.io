TL;DR

The major indices closed higher on September 2, with the S&P 500 up +0.46% to 7,666.60 and the Dow Jones gaining +0.56% to 53,061.95. The VIX fell sharply ‑6.98% to 15.20, reflecting reduced market anxiety.

Sector breadth was broad, led by financials (XLF +0.80%) and materials (XLB +1.69%). The standout stock was NVDA, which jumped +3.21% after beating Q3 expectations.
