TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,666.45, up +0.19%, while the Nasdaq 100 added +0.31% on solid earnings from NVDA and a rebound in energy stocks. 10‑year Treasury yields slipped to 5.24%, reflecting renewed bets on a Fed rate‑cut cycle, yet the market remains vulnerable to a surprise policy tightening.

Sector breadth was mixed: financials and industrials posted modest gains, but consumer discretionary and health care lagged, signaling a tentative rotation toward cyclical exposure. The VIX held at 16.39, a level that historically precedes modest volatility spikes.

Our stance: maintain a core long bias on high‑quality growth names, but trim exposure to over‑extended mega‑caps and watch for a pull‑back if the 10‑year yield rebounds above 5.30%.

  • Broad market up modestly; tech and energy drive gains.
  • Yield curve flattening; 10‑year at 5.24% suggests easing.
  • Watch for volatility resurgence if VIX breaches 18.
