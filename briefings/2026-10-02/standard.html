US equities edge higher as yields retreat and energy gains

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

U.S. equities posted modest gains across the board, driven by a retreat in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 5.24% and a bounce in energy prices after crude slipped below $93. The Nasdaq 100 outperformed the broader market, buoyed by a +1.09% rise in NVDA and continued strength in cloud‑focused XLK.

Behind the headline numbers, the market is re‑pricing the risk of a prolonged rate‑cut cycle. The VIX held near its 2024 low at 16.39, suggesting complacency, while crypto rallied on the back of a weaker dollar. Investors should watch for any surprise in upcoming CPI and PCE releases that could reignite rate‑sensitivity.
