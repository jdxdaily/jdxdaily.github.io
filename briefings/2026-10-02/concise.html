US equities inch higher as yields dip and energy rallies

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

The major indices closed modestly higher on Tuesday, with the S&P 500 up +0.19% at 7,666.45 and the Nasdaq 100 gaining +0.31% to 30,501.56. The rally was anchored by a 1.9% jump in energy ETFs and a 0.8% dip in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 5.24%.

Key earnings: NVDA posted a 1.1% gain after beating Q3 forecasts, while AVGO fell 2.2% on a weaker outlook. Investors watch upcoming Fed minutes and the upcoming earnings of JPM and GS.
