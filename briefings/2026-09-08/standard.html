US equities slip as yields rise, tech leads modest rally

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,718.60, down -0.38%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield climbed to 4.78% (+0.46%). Higher rates pressured consumer‑discretionary and financials, while the Nasdaq‑100 managed a modest gain on continued strength in AI‑related names.

Behind the headline, the VIX spiked to 15.30 (+5.30%), reflecting renewed volatility after the Fed’s “higher‑for‑longer” tone. Commodity price moves—WTI up +1.99% and gold up +0.84%—signal inflation concerns that could keep pressure on equities into the week.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
