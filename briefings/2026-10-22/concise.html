A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,757.17, up +0.58%, while the Nasdaq 100 surged +1.13% on strong earnings from NVDA and a rebound in technology ETFs. 10‑year Treasury yields rose to 5.15%, pushing the VIX down to 16.68 as risk appetite improved.

Energy prices jumped +1.85% to 98.95 on supply concerns, offsetting a modest dip in gold. Investors should watch upcoming earnings from AAPL and MSFT, and any Fed commentary on the policy rate.
