US equities rally on tech earnings, while yields climb

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 posted double‑digit gains, driven by strong earnings from NVDA and TSLA. The rally lifted the broader market, but a higher 10‑year Treasury yield at 5.15% and a firmer dollar signal lingering inflation pressure.

Behind the headline numbers, the tech sector outperformed financials, and the VIX fell below 17, indicating reduced near‑term volatility. However, the modest rise in the DXY and a still‑elevated VIX baseline keep risk‑on sentiment vulnerable to any surprise on inflation data later this week.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Core CPI coming in above 0.3% could reignite rate‑hike expectations.
  • Unexpected dip in oil below 90 USD per barrel would hurt energy‑heavy indexes.
  • Escalation of Middle‑East conflict leading to supply shock.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • AI‑related revenue guidance from AMD or ASML beating consensus.
  • Retail sales surprise to the upside, supporting consumer‑discretionary.
  • Eurozone inflation data showing a clear downtrend, easing dollar pressure.

Positioning notes

  • Tech exposure remains overweight; AI chip names are leading the rally.
  • Rate‑sensitive sectors (real estate, utilities) should be trimmed as yields rise.
  • Maintain a modest hedge in volatility (VIX futures) given the VIX’s low base.
