US equities rally on tech strength as yields climb

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,757.17, up +0.58%, driven by a +2.54% jump in NVDA and a broad‑based tech surge. 10‑year Treasury yields rose to 5.15%, pressuring rate‑sensitive sectors but leaving growth‑oriented names in favour.

Liquidity remains ample: the VIX fell to 16.68 (-3.64%), while the DXY slipped to 101.58 (-0.20%). Commodity markets showed mixed signals—WTI crude up +1.85% at 98.95, gold down -1.15% at 4,102.40.

Our view: Expect continued upside for the Nasdaq‑100 as AI‑related earnings roll out, but watch for a pull‑back if yields breach 5.25% or the VIX rebounds above 18.

  • Tech and AI stocks leading the rally; monitor NVDA, TSLA, META.
  • Yield curve steepening raises risk for high‑beta names; keep an eye on JPM and GS.
  • Volatility low, but a sudden spike could trigger rapid profit‑taking across growth stocks.
