US equities rally on strong earnings, yields ease

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

The major indices closed higher on Thursday, with the S&P 500 up +0.51% to 7,743.41 and the Dow gaining +0.93% to 51,828.62. Broad‑based buying was led by financials, industrials and technology, while the VIX fell to 14.87, its lowest level in three weeks.

Yield pressure eased as the 10‑year Treasury slipped to 5.18%, supporting risk assets. Investors now focus on upcoming earnings from the “Big Tech” cohort and the Fed’s policy outlook after the latest CPI release.
