US equities rally on tech earnings and easing inflation data

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,743.41, up +0.51%, driven by a second‑day surge in mega‑cap tech after MSFT and NVDA posted better‑than‑expected earnings. The rally spilled into the Dow Jones (+0.93%) and Nasdaq‑100 (+0.42%), while the VIX fell to 14.87, the lowest level since early 2025, signalling reduced near‑term volatility.

Behind the headline numbers, the market is pricing in a softer inflation outlook: the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.18% (+0.43%) and the DXY weakened to 100.97 (‑0.32%). Commodity weakness (WTI ‑2.33%) and a modest rise in gold (+0.54%) reinforce the view that the Fed may pause rate hikes after the September meeting.

MSFT delivered revenue of $78.3 bn, beating the consensus of $76.9 bn by +1.8%. EPS came in at $2.71 versus $2.58 expected (+5.0%). The cloud segment grew 23% YoY, driven by AI‑enhanced Azure services.

Pre‑market & overnight

US futures: S&P 500 +0.34%, Nasdaq +0.28%, Dow +0.61%. Asia closed higher, with the Hang Seng up +0.45% on tech gains. European markets mixed; FTSE 100 ‑0.12%, DAX +0.18%. Crypto remains bullish: BTC +0.63%, ETH +0.66%.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • US CPI coming in above 0.3% MoM, reigniting rate‑hike fears.
  • Unexpected drop in Azure bookings, pulling down tech sentiment.
  • Geopolitical escalation in the Middle East that spikes oil above $100 per barrel.
BULLISH TRIGGERS

  • Strong earnings surprise from GOOGL or AMZN reinforcing growth narrative.
  • Resolution of EU‑US trade talks, lifting export‑oriented stocks.
