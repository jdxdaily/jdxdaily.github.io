TL;DR & Editorial Take

The US market extended its gains on Friday, with the SPY up +0.70% and the QQQ up +0.60%. The yield curve steepened, with the 10Y yield rising +1.76% to 4.74. The VIX fell -6.44% to 15.99.

The editorial take is that the market is due for a correction, given the recent run-up in stocks and the steepening yield curve. However, the technicals and options data suggest that the market may continue to grind higher in the short term.

Key points to watch: * The SPY is approaching its 50-day moving average, which could provide resistance. * The QQQ is outperforming the SPY, driven by strength in tech stocks. * The yield curve steepening could be a sign of a growing economy, but also increases the risk of a recession.
