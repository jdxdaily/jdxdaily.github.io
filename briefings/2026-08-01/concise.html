TL;DR

The US stock market closed higher on Thursday, with the Nasdaq 100 leading the gains with a +0.60% increase. The S&P 500 and Dow Jones also rose, with gains of +0.70% and +0.53%, respectively.

The market's upward momentum was driven by the tech sector, with MSFT and NVDA rising +3.02% and +2.93%, respectively. However, the Russell 2000 fell -0.50%, and the VIX declined -6.44%.
