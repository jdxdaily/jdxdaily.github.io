TL;DR & Editorial Take

The US market saw a +0.70% rise in the S&P 500, led by tech stocks, with NVDA and MSFT gaining +2.93% and +3.02% respectively. The VIX dropped to 15.99, down -6.44%. Key takeaways include:

  • The 10Y yield rose to 4.74, up +1.76%, indicating a potential shift in market sentiment.
  • Sector internals show a mixed picture, with XLY and XLC leading gains, while XLP and XLV lagged.
  • Options and positioning data indicate a bullish bias, with +0.20% and +0.85% gains in BTC and ETH respectively.

The market's focus on tech and growth stocks, combined with the drop in the VIX, suggests a risk-on sentiment. However, the rise in the 10Y yield and mixed sector internals indicate a more nuanced picture.

As we look ahead to the week, key events include the release of economic data and earnings reports from major companies. The market's reaction to these events will be crucial in determining the direction of the market.
