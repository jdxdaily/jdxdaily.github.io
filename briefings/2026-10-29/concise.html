TL;DR

The major indices closed higher on Friday, with the S&P 500 up +0.28% to 7,743.98 and the Nasdaq 100 gaining +0.55% to 29,813.16. Yield pressure eased, the 10‑year Treasury fell to 5.05%, supporting risk assets.

Key drivers were a better‑than‑expected earnings beat from AVGO (+2.77%) and a modest dip in the VIX to 16.91. Watch for upcoming CPI data and the Fed’s policy outlook later this week.
