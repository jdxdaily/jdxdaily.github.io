TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,743.98, up +0.28%, driven by a +0.55% Nasdaq 100 and solid earnings from AVGO and GS. 10‑year Treasury yields jumped +1.04% to 5.05%, reflecting renewed expectations of a late‑year rate hike. The VIX fell ‑3.48% to 16.91, indicating lower near‑term fear.

Our editorial view: the market is in a short‑term bullish phase, but the upside is capped by the upside‑risk of a Fed tightening cycle and lingering geopolitical stress in the Middle East. Investors should stay weighted toward resilient tech and financials while trimming exposure to energy and consumer discretionary.

Key take‑aways:

  • Tech earnings beat expectations, pushing the Nasdaq 100 above 30,000 next week.
  • Yield curve steepening suggests a +25bp probability of a 25‑bp Fed hike in December.
  • Volatility contraction (‑3.5% VIX) may lure risk‑averse capital, but also raises the risk of a sharp reversal on any adverse data.
