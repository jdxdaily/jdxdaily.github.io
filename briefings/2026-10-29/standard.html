US equities rally on tech earnings, yields slip

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The major indices closed higher on Thursday, led by the NVDA earnings beat and a modest pull‑back in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 5.05 %. The Nasdaq 100 outperformed with a +0.55% gain, while the VIX fell ‑3.48%, signalling reduced near‑term volatility.

Behind the headline numbers, the market is digesting a widening yield curve and a modestly stronger dollar. The tech sector’s resilience is offset by lagging consumer discretionary ETFs, suggesting a nascent rotation toward defensive and financials ahead of the upcoming earnings wave.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Unexpected rise in CPI (core) above 3.2% YoY could force another rate hike.
