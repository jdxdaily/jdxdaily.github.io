US equities slip marginally as yields fall and energy rallies

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

The major U.S. indices edged lower on Friday, with the S&P 500 at 7,805.45 (-0.02%) and the Nasdaq 100 down -0.31%. The dip came as the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.22%, easing pressure on growth stocks.

Energy prices jumped, lifting the XLE ETF +1.80% after WTI settled at 94.53. Crypto rallied sharply, with Bitcoin up +2.00% and Ethereum +2.36%, while the VIX rose modestly to 15.27.
