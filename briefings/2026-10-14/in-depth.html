TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed flat at 7,805.45, while the Nasdaq 100 fell -0.31% and the Dow slipped -0.35%. Higher‑than‑expected 10‑year Treasury yields (now 5.22%) pressured growth stocks, but the energy sector rallied on a +1.8% jump in WTI crude.

Crypto assets surged, with Bitcoin up +2.0% and Ethereum up +2.36%, reflecting renewed risk appetite despite a modest rise in the VIX (+0.39%). The market remains in a tight range, and the next catalyst will be the Fed’s November policy meeting.

Our stance: maintain a defensive bias on high‑beta tech, tilt toward defensive sectors (Utilities, Health Care) and consider crypto exposure as a tactical overlay.

  • Yield pressure likely to keep growth stocks under pressure through Q4.
  • Energy and commodities present short‑term upside as oil steadies above $95.
  • Crypto volatility offers opportunistic entry points for risk‑on players.
