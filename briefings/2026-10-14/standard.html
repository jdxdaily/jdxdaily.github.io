US equities slip as yields rise and energy gains

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

All major indices edged lower on Thursday, with the S&P 500 down 0.02% and the Nasdaq 100 off 0.31%. The slide reflects a modest re‑pricing of the 10‑year Treasury after the yield fell to 5.22% and a renewed focus on energy as XLE rallied +1.80% on higher crude.

Behind the headline, the market is being pulled apart: technology remains resilient on strong earnings from NVDA and META, while financials and health‑care are under pressure from a weaker dollar and higher rates. The VIX held near 15, suggesting limited panic but a watchful stance ahead of next week’s FOMC minutes.
