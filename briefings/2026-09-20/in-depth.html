US equities edge higher as tech rebounds, yields climb

The full picture: macro, technicals, options, analyst moves, sector internals, international context, and a full week ahead.

20-minute read · Topics: All of the above, plus technicals, options, ratings, week-ahead Download PDF

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 nudged up +0.17% to 7,650.50 while the Nasdaq 100 surged +0.67%, driven by a rebound in semiconductor stocks and a modest easing of inflation concerns. The 10‑year Treasury yield breached the 5% threshold, signaling continued pressure on rate‑sensitive sectors.

Broad market breadth remains thin; only 6 of 11 sector ETFs posted gains, and the VIX fell to 14.81, its lowest level in three months, reflecting reduced near‑term volatility expectations. Nonetheless, the dollar index held steady at 100.22, keeping import‑price pressures alive.

Our editorial stance is cautiously bullish on growth‑oriented tech, while remaining wary of a potential yield‑driven pullback in consumer‑discretionary and financials. Investors should monitor the 10‑year yield’s trajectory and any fresh data on core inflation.

  • Tech leadership: NVDA up +1.34%, AVGO up +2.97%.
  • Yield pressure: 10‑year at 5.00%, up +1.03% on the day.
  • Volatility outlook: VIX down -4.08% to 14.81, suggesting a calm near‑term market.
