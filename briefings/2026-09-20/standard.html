US equities edge higher as tech rallies, yields climb

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 nudged up +0.17% to 7,650.50 while the Nasdaq 100 surged +0.67% to 29,644.17. The rally was driven by a second‑day lift in semiconductor and cloud stocks, offset by a modest pull‑back in consumer‑discretionary and communication services.

Behind the headline, the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped +1.03% to 5.00%, pressuring rate‑sensitive sectors. The VIX fell to 14.81, indicating waning fear. Investors should watch the Fed’s upcoming policy guidance and the earnings calendar for tech giants, which could swing momentum.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Unexpected acceleration in core CPI could force the Fed to tighten further.
  • Sharp correction in AI‑chip valuations if demand stalls.
  • Geopolitical escalation in the Middle East that spikes oil prices.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
