TL;DR & Editorial Take

The US stock market extended its gains on Thursday, with the SPY closing up +0.62% and the QQQ gaining +1.19%. The rally was led by the tech sector, with NVDA and TSLA both posting significant gains.

Our editorial take is that the market is currently in a bullish trend, driven by strong earnings reports and a favorable macroeconomic environment. However, we also note that the market is due for a correction, and investors should be cautious of potential risks.
