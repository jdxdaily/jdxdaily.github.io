US equities tumble as rates rise and tech stalls

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 slipped -1.09% to 7,659.44, driven by a broad tech sell‑off and a 10‑year Treasury yield that nudged up to 5.10%. The VIX surged +21.45%, underscoring heightened volatility ahead of the Fed’s policy decision.

Energy rallied on a +2.52% jump in XLE as crude breached the $100 mark, while defensive sectors (XLP, XLU, XLB) posted modest gains, suggesting investors are re‑balancing toward safety ahead of potential rate hikes.
