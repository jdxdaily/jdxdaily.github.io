TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,631.47, down -0.71%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped to 4.80% (+0.80%). Higher rates and a 9.5% surge in the VIX underscore growing risk aversion.

Technology led the decline, with the Nasdaq 100 off -1.29%. Defensive sectors showed modest resilience; energy rallied on higher crude, while consumer discretionary lagged.

We remain cautious ahead of the Fed’s September meeting. Expect continued intra‑day volatility, especially in high‑beta names, and watch for any surprise in the upcoming earnings season.

  • Yield curve steepening pushes growth stocks lower.
  • Energy ETFs post the only sector‑wide gain.
  • VIX breakout signals heightened short‑term hedging demand.
