US equities slip as yields rise and volatility spikes

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The major indices closed lower, with the S&P 500 down -0.77% and the Nasdaq 100 off -1.08%. Higher Treasury yields and a jump in the VIX to 16.07 signal renewed risk aversion, pressuring growth‑heavy tech stocks.

Despite the broad sell‑off, NVDA posted a +1.68% gain, buoyed by a strong earnings preview, while defensive sectors such as utilities and consumer staples showed modest resilience. Investors should watch the Fed’s next policy cue and the upcoming earnings wave for potential catalysts.

What could break the tape

  • Unexpected acceleration in CPI above 0.3% MoM.

  • Weak Q3 earnings from AI‑exposed names (NVDA, AMD).
