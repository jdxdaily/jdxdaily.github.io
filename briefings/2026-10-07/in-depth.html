TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,818.93, up +0.58%, as earnings beat expectations and the 10‑year Treasury slipped to 5.27%.

Technology led the upside, with NVDA and AVGO posting double‑digit gains on chip‑supply news, while defensive sectors lagged.

Liquidity remains ample; the dollar index rose to 102.09 and the VIX fell to 15.01, suggesting a low‑volatility environment that could reverse if inflation data surprise to the upside.

  • Broad market breadth remains positive: 9 of 11 sector ETFs closed higher.
  • Yield curve flattening continues – 2‑year at 4.85% versus 10‑year at 5.27%.
  • Watch for a pull‑back in tech if the NVDA rally stalls ahead of its Q4 guidance.
