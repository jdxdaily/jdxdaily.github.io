US equities rally on tech earnings, while rate cuts stay out of reach

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed above 7,800 for the first time this month, driven by a broad tech bounce after NVDA and AVGO posted better‑than‑expected guidance. Momentum is reinforced by a 15.01 VIX, the lowest level since June, indicating waning fear.

Despite the rally, the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.27, keeping the Fed’s next rate‑cut window beyond the near term. The combination of a softer yield curve and a firming dollar (102.09 DXY) creates a mixed backdrop for emerging‑market inflows.
