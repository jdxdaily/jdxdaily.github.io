US equities rally on lower yields, earnings beat lifts tech

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

Broad equity gains were led by the S&P 500 up +0.58% to 7,818.93 and the Nasdaq 100 rising +0.48% to 31,224.47. The rally was underpinned by a -0.79% pull‑back in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 5.27% and a strong earnings beat from AVGO.

Defensive sectors lagged; the Russell 2000 slipped -0.59% and the VIX fell to 15.01, indicating easing volatility. Investors should watch upcoming earnings and the Fed’s policy outlook for directional cues.
