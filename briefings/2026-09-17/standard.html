US equities slip as yields rise and energy drags

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 fell -0.45% to 7,551.81, driven by a broad sell‑off in financials and energy as the 10‑year Treasury yield nudged above 5 %. The Nasdaq held near flat, buoyed by modest gains in chip‑maker NVDA and cloud‑software stocks, but the overall market breadth remains thin.

Behind the headline numbers, the VIX spiked +2.97% to 17.71, signalling rising risk aversion. Crypto rallied, with Bitcoin up +0.90%, while gold slipped -1.29%, reflecting a shift toward dollar‑linked assets amid higher yields.
