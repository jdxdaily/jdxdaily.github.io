US equities rally on tech strength as yields ease

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,722.72, up +0.73%, driven by a broad tech bounce and a modest retreat in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 5.28%. Momentum is reinforced by a sharp VIX decline to 15.31 (‑6.59%), suggesting risk appetite is returning.

While the rally appears robust, the market remains vulnerable to two headwinds: (1) a potential surprise rate hike if core inflation stalls, and (2) geopolitical escalation in the Middle East that could spike energy prices. Investors should watch the 10‑year yield’s 200‑day moving average (5.35%) and the VIX term structure for early warning signs.

Our stance: maintain a core‑plus equity bias, tilt toward high‑growth tech, but hedge with short‑dated volatility or Treasury futures to protect against a rapid risk‑off.

  • Tech ETFs lead with +1.01% for XLK; semiconductor exposure remains the top relative performer.
  • Yield curve flattening continues; 2‑year at 5.12% versus 10‑year at 5.28%.
  • VIX term structure shows a steep contango, indicating cheap near‑term protection.
