US equities rally on earnings beat, yields ease

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

All major US indices closed higher on Friday, with the S&P 500 up +0.73% to 7,722.72 and the Nasdaq 100 gaining +1.00% to 30,807.93. The rally was anchored by strong earnings from NVDA and AVGO, while the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.28%.

Volatility receded, with the VIX down -6.59% to 15.31. Commodity prices fell, led by WTI crude at 89.91 USD, supporting risk‑on sentiment.
