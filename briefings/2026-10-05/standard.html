US equities rally on tech earnings, yields slip

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,722.72, up +0.73%, driven by a broad tech bounce after strong earnings from NVDA and AVGO. The 10‑year Treasury yield fell to 5.28, easing pressure on growth stocks and narrowing the yield curve steepness.

Behind the headline numbers, the VIX collapsed to 15.31 (-6.59%), signaling a sharp drop in market volatility. However, the modest rise in the dollar index (102.29) and a firmer dollar could re‑price commodity‑linked equities, especially energy and materials, later in the week.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Unexpected rise in CPI above 3.2% could reignite rate‑hike expectations.
  • Sharp drop in crude below $85 could hurt energy‑heavy indices.
  • Escalation of U.S.–China tech restrictions, especially on semiconductor equipment.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • Better‑than‑expected ISM manufacturing (≥49) signaling economic resilience.
  • Continuation of AI‑chip demand, pushing NVDA and peers to new highs.
  • Eurozone fiscal stimulus rollout boosting global growth outlook.

Positioning notes
