US Stocks Extend Gains as Tech Leads, VIX Slips Below 15

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The US market extended its gains from the previous session, with the SPY closing up +0.62% and the QQQ up +1.19%. The VIX slipped below 15, indicating a decrease in market volatility. Key takeaways include:

  • The tech sector led the gains, with NVDA and TSLA up +2.27% and +2.83% respectively.
  • The 10-year yield fell -0.21% to 4.66, while WTI crude rose +1.15% to 78.18.
  • The DXY index fell -0.37% to 99.60, indicating a decrease in the value of the US dollar.

Our editorial take is that the market is currently in a bullish trend, driven by the strong performance of the tech sector. However, investors should remain cautious and keep an eye on the VIX and other volatility indicators.

The market's gains were broad-based, with all major indices closing in the green. The DOW was up +0.28%, while the RUT was up +1.10%.
