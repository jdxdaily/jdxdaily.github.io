TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,623.68, down -0.52%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped to 4.99% (+0.71%). Energy rallied on a +3.82% surge in WTI, while financials and consumer discretionary lagged.

Higher yields compress equity valuations and pressure rate‑sensitive sectors. The VIX rose to 19.45, indicating growing near‑term volatility amid mixed macro signals.

Our stance: stay defensive on high‑beta names, seek quality exposure in health care and utilities, and monitor the 10‑year yield for a potential breakout above 5.00% which could trigger broader sell‑offs.
