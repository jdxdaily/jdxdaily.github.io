US markets edge lower as oil slides and yields rise

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The US equity market closed muted, with the Russell 2000 up +0.52% to 2,875.36 while the major indices posted no data due to reporting lag. The market’s underlying tone was bearish: the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 4.96% (‑0.70%), and WTI crude fell ‑5.76% to 89.14, pressuring energy‑heavy stocks.

Liquidity remains tight ahead of the Fed’s next policy meeting. The dollar index rose to 100.71 (+0.27%), supporting import‑dependent sectors and keeping gold under pressure despite a modest rise to 4,379.10. Crypto showed limited upside, with BTC up +0.18% and ETH down ‑0.36%.
