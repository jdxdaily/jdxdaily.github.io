US markets steady as rate cuts loom, oil slides and crypto steadies

The full picture: macro, technicals, options, analyst moves, sector internals, international context, and a full week ahead.

20-minute read · Topics: All of the above, plus technicals, options, ratings, week-ahead Download PDF

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The US equity market closed largely unchanged on Friday, with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq still awaiting fresh direction. The 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 4.96%, reinforcing expectations of a 25 bp Fed cut at the November meeting. Energy prices retreated sharply; WTI fell ‑5.8% to 89.14, dragging the energy sector lower.

Risk sentiment remains mixed. The VIX rose modestly to 14.87, while the DXY edged higher, suggesting a cautious bias toward the dollar. Crypto assets showed limited movement, with BTC up +0.2% and ETH down ‑0.4%.

Our view: stay selective on high‑quality growth names, watch for a breakout in the technology sector, and consider defensive positioning in utilities and consumer staples if the market tests the 4,200 SPY support.

  • Expect the Fed to trim rates in November; price this into equity valuations.
  • Monitor oil inventory data for a potential rebound in energy ETFs.
  • Watch the VIX term structure for signs of a volatility spike ahead of earnings season.
