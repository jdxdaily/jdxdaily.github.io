US equities rally on earnings beat, while yields edge higher

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

The major indices closed sharply higher on Friday, with the S&P 500 up +0.59% at 7,811.54 and the Dow Jones gaining +0.83% to 51,654.95. Earnings surprises from AMZN and MSFT drove the upside, while the 10‑year Treasury yield rose to 5.24%, keeping the market’s risk appetite in check.

Volatility eased, with the VIX down -3.70% at 14.84. The dollar slipped marginally and commodities were mixed, leaving the broader macro backdrop relatively neutral.
