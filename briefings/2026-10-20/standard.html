US equities rally on tech strength, but rate worries linger

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The Nasdaq 100 surged +1.17% to 29,529.35 as NVDA and AMZN posted double‑digit gains, pulling the broader market higher. Meanwhile, the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped +0.75% to 5.14%, keeping the risk‑off sentiment alive and pressuring financials.

Behind the headline numbers, the rally is driven by a sharp rebound in semiconductor earnings expectations and a dip in oil prices that eases inflation concerns. Yet the upside is capped by a still‑elevated yield curve and a modest pullback in the dollar, suggesting volatility could return if rates climb further.
