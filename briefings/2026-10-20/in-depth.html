US equities rally on tech earnings, but rate‑rise risk looms

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The Nasdaq‑100 surged +1.2% after Amazon’s earnings beat and Nvidia’s continued AI‑chip demand, pulling the S&P 500 to a fresh 7‑month high. However, the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped to 5.14%, the highest since early 2023, reviving concerns over monetary tightening.

Energy remains under pressure as WTI slipped below $100, while the VIX fell to 18.44, indicating a calmer risk environment. Yet the dollar index weakened to 101.77, supporting commodity‑linked equities.

Our view: stay weighted toward high‑growth tech and selective defensive plays, but trim exposure to rate‑sensitive financials ahead of the Fed’s next policy meeting.

  • Tech‑heavy ETFs (XLK, QQQ) lead the rally; consider adding NVDA and AMZN on pull‑backs.
  • Financials (XLF, JPM) underperform; reduce net exposure.
  • Monitor 10‑year yield at 5.14% – a breach of 5.20% could trigger broader equity corrections.
