A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,690.73, up +0.41%, while the Nasdaq 100 jumped +1.17% to 29,529.35. Tech giants led the advance, offsetting a modest dip in the Dow Jones (-0.07%).

Energy prices fell sharply, with WTI crude down -4.60% to 98.89, dragging the energy ETF XLE lower. The 10‑year Treasury yield rose to 5.14, supporting the dollar and pressuring commodities.
