US equities rally on rate relief while tech stalls

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 nudged higher to 7,712.57 (+0.28%) as the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.12%, easing pressure on rate‑sensitive sectors. The Dow outperformed with a +1.02% gain, driven by financials and health‑care, while the Nasdaq 100 dipped -0.13% as semiconductor leader NVDA retreated.

Behind the headline numbers, the market is digesting a softer inflation outlook and a modest pullback in oil. The VIX fell to 17.31 (-6.13%), suggesting lower near‑term volatility, but the crypto rally (BTC +0.95%) and a resilient gold price keep risk appetite mixed.

Spotlight: Fed’s dovish pivot and its market impact

The Federal Reserve’s latest policy statement signaled a pause on further rate hikes, citing “moderating inflation pressures.” This shift moved the 10‑year Treasury yield down 0.37% to 5.12%, the steepest decline since March 2024, and compressed the yield curve.
