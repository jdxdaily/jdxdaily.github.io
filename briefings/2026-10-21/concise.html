US equities rise on strong earnings, while oil slides

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

The Dow Jones surged +1.02% to 52,495.88, pulling the broader market higher as financials and health‑care led gains. Meanwhile, the Nasdaq slipped -0.13% amid a modest pullback in technology, and oil prices fell sharply on weaker demand outlook.

Key macro data showed the 10‑year Treasury yield easing to 5.12% and the VIX retreating to 17.31, indicating reduced volatility risk. Investors now focus on upcoming earnings from the “Magnificent Seven” and Fed policy cues.
