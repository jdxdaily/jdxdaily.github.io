US equities slip as tech drags, meta spikes on earnings beat

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,636.36, down -0.48%, as broad weakness in technology outweighed a rare surge in META (+6.55%) after its earnings beat. The VIX jumped +4.71% to 16.46, signaling heightened short‑term risk appetite.

Behind the numbers, the 10‑year Treasury yield rose to 4.84% (++0.65%), pressuring growth valuations, while energy ETFs gained on a modest dip in WTI. Investors should watch for further Fed commentary and the upcoming earnings season, which could reset market direction.
