US equities slip as yields rise and volatility spikes

The full picture: macro, technicals, options, analyst moves, sector internals, international context, and a full week ahead.

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,636.36, down -0.48%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield climbed to 4.84% (+0.65%). Higher rates and a jump in the VIX to 16.46 (+4.71%) reignited risk aversion, pulling most sectors lower.

Technology remains the only bright spot, led by META which surged +6.55% on strong ad‑revenue guidance. Defensive sectors lagged, with financials (XLF) and industrials (XLI) posting sub‑1% declines.

We view today’s pull‑back as a tactical entry for quality growth and dividend payers, but remain wary of further upside‑rate pressure and a potentially prolonged volatility regime.

  • Rate‑sensitive stocks under pressure as 10‑year yields breach 4.8%.
  • VIX breakout suggests higher near‑term volatility; monitor 0‑DTE put flow.
  • Meta’s outsize rally offers a short‑term catalyst, but broader tech still faces valuation headwinds.
