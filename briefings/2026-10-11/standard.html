US equities rally on tech earnings, while yields edge higher

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,811.54, up +0.59%, driven by a broad tech bounce after MSFT and AMZN posted better‑than‑expected earnings. The rally was underpinned by a modest decline in the VIX to 14.84 and a stable 10‑year Treasury yield at 5.24, suggesting risk appetite remains intact.

However, the market faces two near‑term headwinds: a still‑elevated inflation backdrop reflected in gold’s +1.43% rise and a modest pullback in energy ETFs. Investors should watch the Fed’s upcoming policy guidance and the earnings of AI‑heavy names for potential volatility spikes.

What could break the tape
