TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,811.54, up +0.59%, driven by strong earnings from AMZN and a softer inflation outlook that nudged the 10‑year yield to 5.24%.

Sector breadth remains healthy: eight of eleven sector ETFs posted gains, while the VIX slipped to 14.84, the lowest level since early 2025, signaling reduced near‑term volatility.

Risk remains on the downside from a potential Fed rate‑cut surprise and geopolitical tension in the Middle East, which could reignite commodity‑price pressure.

  • Broad market rally anchored by tech and consumer discretionary earnings.
  • Yield curve flattening continues; 10‑year at 5.24% versus 2‑year at 4.78% (data not shown).
  • Watch for a breakout above 8,000 on the S&P 500 as a bullish confirmation.
