US equities rally on strong earnings and easing inflation data

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 surged +0.86% to 7,656.98, driven by a broad earnings beat wave and a sharp pull‑back in the VIX to 15.84. Momentum is underpinned by a firmer 10‑year Treasury at 4.97% and a modest rise in the DXY, suggesting confidence in a still‑tight monetary stance.

Behind the headline numbers, technology remains the engine, with the NVDA rally stalling while AAPL and AMZN post double‑digit gains. The modest dip in crypto and a flattening yield curve hint at a near‑term risk‑off pivot if inflation surprises to the upside.

Macro & Fed

The FOMC left rates unchanged at 5.25‑5.50% and signaled a “patient” stance, noting inflation at 2.8% YoY—down from 3.1% a month ago. The yield curve remains flat, with the 2‑year at 5.12% and the 10‑year at 4.97%.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Unexpected CPI surge above 0.3% could reignite rate‑hike expectations.
  • Escalation of Middle‑East conflict leading to a second oil shock.
  • Weakening of the US dollar below 98.0 DXY, pressuring import‑heavy equities.
  • Tech earnings miss, especially from AI leaders, triggering sector rotation.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • Further AI earnings beats pushing the Nasdaq above 30,000.
  • Continued decline in VIX below 14.0, reinforcing risk‑on sentiment.
  • Positive surprise in US CPI, confirming inflation is on a sustainable downtrend.
  • Resolution of US‑China semiconductor talks, unlocking supply‑chain upside.

Positioning notes

  • Maintain a core long bias in technology and financials while trimming utilities as yields rise.
  • Allocate a modest tactical exposure to energy ETFs (XLE) to capture the oil rally.
  • Keep a defensive cash buffer for potential volatility spikes if inflation data disappoints.
