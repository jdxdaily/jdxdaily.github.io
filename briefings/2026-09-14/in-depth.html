US equities rally on strong earnings and easing inflation risk

The full picture: macro, technicals, options, analyst moves, sector internals, international context, and a full week ahead.

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 surged +0.86% to 7,656.98, driven by a cluster of beat‑and‑raise earnings and a retreat in the VIX to 15.84. 10‑year Treasury yields slipped to 4.97%, reinforcing the view that the Fed’s tightening cycle is nearing its end.

Technology led the upside, with the Nasdaq 100 up +0.91%. The market breadth remains healthy—10 of 11 sector ETFs posted gains, and the Russell 2000 added +0.45% on solid small‑cap momentum.

Risk remains on the table: a surprise hawkish comment from the Fed’s Vice‑Chair could reignite rate‑sensitivity, while geopolitical friction in the Middle East threatens energy price volatility.

  • Broad equity rally anchored by earnings strength and lower inflation expectations.
  • Yield curve flattening continues; 10‑year at 4.97%, 2‑year near 5.12%.
  • Watch for Fed commentary and any escalation in Middle‑East tensions as near‑term catalysts.
