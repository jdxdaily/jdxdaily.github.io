US equities rally on strong earnings and easing volatility

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

All three major indices closed higher, with the S&P 500 up +0.86% to 7,656.98 and the Nasdaq 100 gaining +0.91% to 29,368.44. The rally was underpinned by solid earnings from tech giants and a sharp drop in the VIX, which fell ‑11.21% to 15.84.

Energy prices jumped over 2% on the back of a weaker dollar and higher demand, while the 10‑year Treasury yield rose to 4.97%, keeping the risk‑on tone intact.
