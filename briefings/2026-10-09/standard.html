US equities slip as tech drags, energy rallies on oil price dip

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 fell -0.47% and the Nasdaq 100 dropped -1.39% on weaker earnings from mega‑cap tech, while the Dow eked out a modest gain. Energy ETFs led the rally, buoyed by a sub‑$91 WTI price that supports profit margins for oil producers.

Behind the headline numbers, the market is pricing in a higher‑for‑longer rate environment after the latest FOMC minutes hinted at a slower pace of cuts. The VIX’s rise to 15.41 signals growing volatility risk, especially as earnings season intensifies.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Further NVDA guidance below $3.30 could drag the tech sector -2% or more.
  • Unexpected rise in 10‑year yield above 5.30% would pressure growth stocks.
  • Geopolitical escalation in the Middle East raising oil prices sharply.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • Strong earnings surprise from AAPL or JPM could lift the S&P 500 +0.5%.
  • Core PCE coming in under 2.8% would revive rate‑cut optimism.
  • Energy ETFs sustaining >3% gains on a rebound in crude.

Positioning notes

  • Maintain a defensive bias: overweight consumer staples and utilities.
  • Selective long exposure to energy and financials as yields stabilize.
  • Use NVDA and AMD as short‑/long pairs to capture intra‑sector rotation.
