US equities slip as yields rise, VIX spikes

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

The major indices closed lower on Friday, with the S&P 500 down -0.76% to 7,706.03 and the Dow Jones off -1.03% at 51,511.59. The VIX surged +2.08% to 15.18, reflecting heightened volatility as the 10‑year Treasury yield climbed to 5.11%, up +3.04% on the day.

Technology lagged, led by a -1.47% pullback in NVDA, while defensive sectors showed modest gains. Investors remain focused on upcoming earnings and the Fed’s policy outlook.
