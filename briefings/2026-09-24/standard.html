US equities slip as yields rise and tech pressure mounts

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The major indices closed lower, with the S&P 500 down -0.76% and the Dow off -1.03%. The 10‑year Treasury yield jumped +3.04% to 5.11%, lifting the VIX to 15.18 and underscoring heightened risk aversion.

Behind the headline, the tech sector is the primary drag: the Nasdaq 100 barely budged while NVDA fell -1.47% and GOOGL slumped -3.80%. Energy rallied on higher oil, but utilities and consumer discretionary lagged, setting up a classic defensive rotation.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • 10‑year yield breaches 5.20%, tightening financing for growth stocks.
  • VIX spikes above 18, indicating heightened volatility.
  • Unexpected CPI surge > 0.5% MoM, prompting rate‑hike speculation.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • AI‑related revenue beats across NVDA, AMD, and GOOGL.
  • Oil price climbs above 95 USD, lifting energy ETFs.
  • Positive earnings surprise from any of the major tech names.

Positioning notes

  • Shift weight toward defensive sectors (XLP, XLU) as yields stay elevated.
  • Maintain selective exposure to AI leaders with strong balance sheets.
  • Monitor credit spreads; widening spreads may pressure high‑beta names.
