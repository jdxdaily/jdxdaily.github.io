TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,706.03, down -0.76%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped to 5.11% (+3.04%). Higher financing costs pressured consumer‑discretionary and growth stocks, while energy ETFs posted modest gains on a firm‑priced WTI market.

Technical pressure is building on the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100, both testing key support zones around 7,650 and 30,400 respectively. The VIX rose to 15.18 (+2.08%), signalling rising short‑term volatility expectations.

We remain cautious on equity exposure ahead of the Fed’s September meeting. Defensive sectors (utilities, consumer staples) and high‑yield credit appear relatively attractive, while speculative tech bets require tighter risk control.

  • Yield curve steepening: 10‑year at 5.11%, 2‑year near 4.85%.
  • Sector breadth: 5‑up, 6‑down; XLE up +0.96%, XLU down -2.24%.
  • Options flow: 0‑DTE put volume up 42% on SPY, indicating short‑term bearish tilt.
