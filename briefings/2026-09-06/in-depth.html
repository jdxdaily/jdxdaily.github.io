TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,718.60, down -0.38%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield climbed to 4.78% (+0.46%). Defensive sectors lagged, while the Nasdaq 100 eked out a modest gain on +0.21% despite a -2.51% drop in AAPL and a -5.92% slide in TSLA. The market remains caught between higher‑for‑longer rates and lingering inflation concerns.

Liquidity is tightening: 0‑DTE option volume surged 22% on the day, with a net put bias of -12.4%. The VIX rose to 14.53, indicating modest volatility expectations.

Our view: focus on quality large‑cap tech with resilient cash flows, while maintaining defensive positioning in financials and utilities until the Fed signals a clearer rate trajectory.

  • Yield pressure likely to keep the S&P 500 under 7,800 through Q4.
  • Tech earnings season starts next week; watch NVDA and META for upside catalysts.
  • Consider hedging with short‑dated VIX calls or protective puts on SPY as volatility rebounds.
