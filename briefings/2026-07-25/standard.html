Risk-Off Sentiment Returns as US Yields and Dollar Rise

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The US market saw a risk-off sentiment return, with the SPY and QQQ experiencing declines due to rising US yields and a stronger dollar. The 10Y Yield rose to 4.68, while the DXY increased to 101.43. This shift in sentiment was also reflected in the price of gold, which rose to 4,067.60, and the price of oil, which fell to 89.31.

Below the surface, the rotation out of growth and into value continued, with the XLE and XLF outperforming. The VIX also saw a decrease, falling to 18.58. As we look ahead to the US open, investors will be watching the Futures closely, as well as the upcoming earnings reports from major companies.
