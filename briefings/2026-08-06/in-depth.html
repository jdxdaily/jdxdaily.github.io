TL;DR & Editorial Take

The US market saw a mixed session, with the Dow Jones rising +0.49% and the S&P 500 falling -0.17%. The Nasdaq 100 underperformed, dropping -0.83%. Gold was the standout performer, surging +7.17% as US yields dipped.

Key takeaways from the session include:

  • The Russell 2000 outperformed, rising +1.22% as small-cap stocks saw a bounce.
  • The VIX fell -4.18%, indicating a decrease in market volatility.
  • WTI crude oil dropped -0.39%, while the DXY rose +0.03%.

Our editorial take is that the market is currently navigating a complex landscape, with conflicting signals from different asset classes. While gold's surge may indicate a flight to safety, the rise in the Dow Jones and Russell 2000 suggests that risk appetite remains intact.
