TL;DR

The US market saw a mixed session, with the Dow Jones rising 0.49% and the Nasdaq 100 falling 0.83%. Below the surface, gold's 7.17% surge and the 4.18% drop in the VIX suggest a shift in investor sentiment. The Russell 2000's 1.22% gain indicates a rotation into smaller-cap stocks.

The rally in gold and decline in US yields may be a sign of decreasing risk appetite, but the 1.66% and 2.12% gains in BTC and ETH suggest otherwise. As we head into the US open, keep an eye on sector rotation, with XLV and XLB outperforming.
