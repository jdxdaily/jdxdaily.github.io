TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,591.70, down -0.58%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped +2.21% to 4.94%. Higher rates and a surging VIX (+8.38%) signal renewed risk aversion.

Technology remains the weakest link; the Nasdaq 100 fell -1.08% and chip‑maker NVDA slid -2.26%. Defensive sectors showed modest resilience, with communications services up +0.60%.

Liquidity is tightening. Overnight flows show a net sell of 0‑DTE calls, while the VIX term structure steepens, suggesting market participants are buying protection.

  • Yield curve steepening raises concerns for growth‑sensitive equities.
  • VIX surge and 0‑DTE put buying point to near‑term downside bias.
  • Watch for earnings surprises from AAPL and GOOGL as catalysts.
