US equities slip as yields rise and volatility spikes

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

Broad equity sell‑off persisted on Thursday, with the S&P 500 down -0.58% and the Nasdaq 100 off -1.08%. The move was anchored by a jump in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 4.94% (+2.21%) and a surge in the VIX to 17.84 (+8.38%), underscoring heightened risk aversion.

Despite the broad weakness, defensive and communication stocks showed resilience, while chip‑related names such as NVDA fell sharply. The market’s near‑term direction will hinge on upcoming earnings, the Fed’s policy stance, and geopolitical friction in the Middle East.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • US CPI > 0.4% MoM, reinforcing rate‑hike expectations.
  • Further escalation in Middle‑East oil supply risk.
  • NVDA quarterly miss confirming AI demand slowdown.
  • Unexpected weakness in US retail sales.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • Core CPI < 0.2% MoM, opening path for rate cuts.
  • Strong earnings beat from AAPL or AMZN.
  • Resolution of Middle‑East tensions, easing oil markets.
  • Positive surprise in durable goods orders.

Positioning notes

  • Risk‑off bias is evident; consider overweighting defensive sectors (XLP, XLU).
  • Tech exposure should be trimmed; focus on earnings‑driven names rather than pure growth.
  • Monitor yield curve flattening – a steeper inversion could accelerate rate‑cut expectations.
