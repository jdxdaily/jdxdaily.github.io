US equities tumble as yields rise and volatility spikes

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

All three major indices closed lower on September 10, with the S&P 500 down -0.58% at 7,591.70 and the Nasdaq 100 slipping -1.08% to 29,103.51. The 10‑year Treasury yield jumped to 4.94%, its highest since early 2024, while the VIX surged +8.38% to 17.84, underscoring heightened risk appetite.

Tech heavyweight NVDA led losers, down -2.26%, while AAPL posted a rare +3.56% rally on a new iPhone forecast. Investors will watch Friday’s CPI release and upcoming earnings from the “Big Tech” cohort.
