US equities rally on rate relief, but tech stalls

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 nudged higher to 7,744.06 (+0.33%) as the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.09%, easing financing pressures on rate‑sensitive sectors. Financials (XLF) and health care (XLV) led the gains, while the Nasdaq‑100 lagged, pulling the broader market into a modest, uneven rally.

Behind the headline, the market is digesting a mixed earnings backdrop and a modest pullback in semiconductor exposure, highlighted by NVDA down -1.84%. The combination of a softer yield curve and a still‑elevated VIX at 16.46 suggests volatility could re‑emerge if macro data disappoints.

Spotlight: rate curve easing lifts financials

The 10‑year Treasury yield fell -1.95% to 5.09%, the steepest drop since June. This move sharpened the spread between 2‑year and 10‑year yields, reviving expectations of a Fed rate pause.
