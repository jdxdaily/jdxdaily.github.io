US equities rise on strong Dow, while tech stalls

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

The Dow Jones surged +1.08% to 52,177.69, pulling the broader market higher as the S&P 500 nudged +0.33% to 7,744.06. The Nasdaq 100 slipped -0.05% to 29,935.60, reflecting pressure on chip and AI‑related names.

Yield compression continued with the 10‑year Treasury at 5.09% (-1.95%), while commodities fell; WTI crude dropped -2.14% to 95.87. Defensive and cyclical ETFs posted mixed gains, led by financials (+1.38%) and consumer discretionary (+1.71%).
