US equities rally on tech earnings, while volatility retreats

The full picture: macro, technicals, options, analyst moves, sector internals, international context, and a full week ahead.

20-minute read · Topics: All of the above, plus technicals, options, ratings, week-ahead Download PDF

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 surged +1.14% to 7,637.76, driven by a wave of strong Q3 earnings from mega‑caps and a sharp decline in the VIX to 15.44. The rally was underpinned by a modest dip in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 4.95%, reinforcing risk‑on sentiment across the board.

Technology led the charge, with the Nasdaq 100 up +1.73% and sector ETF XLK gaining +2.25%. Defensive sectors lagged, notably communications services (XLC ‑0.58%) as investors rotated into growth.

While the market breadth remains positive, the narrowing VIX and a still‑elevated DXY at 100.29 suggest caution ahead of the Fed’s September meeting. We remain bullish on high‑quality tech, but watch for a pull‑back if inflation data re‑accelerates.

  • Broad equity rally powered by tech earnings and lower rates.
  • Volatility at multi‑month lows; VIX down ‑12.82%.
  • Watch for Fed policy signals and any upside surprise in CPI.
