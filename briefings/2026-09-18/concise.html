US equities rally on tech strength, volatility eases

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

The major indices closed sharply higher on September 17, with the S&P 500 up +1.14% to 7,637.76 and the Nasdaq 100 leading at +1.73% to 29,446.98. The VIX fell -12.82% to 15.44, reflecting reduced risk appetite.

Tech and communication stocks drove the gains, while the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 4.95, supporting equity valuations. No major macro surprises; the market remains focused on upcoming earnings and Fed policy outlook.
