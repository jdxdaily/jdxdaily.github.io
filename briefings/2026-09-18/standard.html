US equities rally on tech earnings, yields slip

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 surged +1.14% to 7,637.76, driven by a wave of strong earnings from mega‑caps and a retreat in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 4.95%. Momentum extended into the Nasdaq 100, which jumped +1.73%, as semiconductor leader NVDA and cloud‑heavy MSFT posted better‑than‑expected results.

Behind the headline numbers, the VIX collapsed ‑12.82% to 15.44, signalling a sharp drop in implied volatility. The only drag came from communication services (XLC –0.58%) and a modest dip in the dollar index, leaving room for further upside if earnings momentum persists.

NVDA delivered a +2.54% close after reporting Q3 revenue of 29.2 billion, up 23% YoY, and EPS of 3.15, beating consensus of 2.97. The company highlighted a 45% YoY increase in AI‑accelerated data‑center sales, reinforcing its position as the sector’s growth engine.

Pre‑market & overnight

US futures: ES +0.9%, NQ +1.3%. Asia markets closed mixed; Japan’s Nikkei down 0.4%, Hong Kong’s Hang Seng up 0.2% on tech gains. Europe: FTSE 100 up 0.5%, DAX 0.7% higher after ECB rate‑watch comments. Crypto: BTC +1.9%, ETH +2.8% on risk‑on sentiment.

Macro & Fed

FOMC minutes showed a majority leaning toward a pause, citing inflation at 2.8% YoY and a still‑elevated but easing labor market. The yield curve remains mildly inverted (2‑year at 5.02% vs 10‑year at 4.95%).

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Unexpected rise in US CPI above 0.4% MoM, reigniting rate‑hike fears.
  • Escalation of Middle‑East conflict that spikes oil prices above $110.
  • Weak Q4 guidance from NVDA or AMD on AI demand.
  • Sudden dollar surge (DXY > 101) pulling capital out of equities.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • Fed signals a longer‑term pause or rate cut.
  • Positive surprise in US CPI (below 0.2% MoM) confirming disinflation.
  • Strong earnings beat from INTC or ORCL on cloud growth.
  • Crypto rally pushes BTC above $80,000, lifting risk appetite.

Positioning notes

  • Tech exposure remains overweight; AI‑centric names lead the rally.
  • Rate‑sensitive financials are neutral pending clearer Fed guidance.
  • Defensive sectors (Utilities, Staples) offer modest upside as a hedge against volatility spikes.
