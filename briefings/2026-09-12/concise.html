US equities rally on strong earnings, volatility eases

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

The major indices closed sharply higher on September 11, with the S&P 500 up +0.86% to 7,656.98 and the Nasdaq 100 gaining +0.91% to 29,368.44. Broad‑based buying was led by technology and financials, while the VIX fell -11.21% to 15.84, indicating reduced risk appetite.

Macro backdrop featured a 10‑year Treasury yield at 4.97% (+0.63%) and oil at 100.05 USD, down -2.37%. No major policy surprises, but earnings momentum remains the key driver.
