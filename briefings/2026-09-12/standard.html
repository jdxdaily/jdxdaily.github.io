US equities rally on strong earnings and easing inflation expectations

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,656.98, up +0.86%, as earnings beat expectations across technology and consumer discretionary, while the VIX fell to 15.84 (-11.21%), signaling reduced risk aversion. Behind the headline gains, the market is pricing in a slower‑than‑expected slowdown in core inflation, keeping the 10‑year Treasury yield under 5% and leaving room for a modest Fed rate‑cut later in the year.

However, the rally is built on thin volume and a narrow set of large‑cap winners. The Nasdaq 100’s +0.91% advance was led by AAPL and AMZN, while defensive sectors lagged, hinting at a potential pull‑back if earnings momentum wanes or macro data surprise to the downside.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Unexpected rise in core CPI above 0.3% MoM could force the Fed to accelerate cuts.
  • Weakening of the Chinese PMI below 48, prompting a broader risk‑off.
  • Geopolitical escalation in the Middle East that spikes oil above $110/barrel.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • Surprise upside in US retail sales (+0.6% MoM) reinforcing consumer strength.
  • Tech earnings beat beyond consensus, especially AI‑chip makers.
  • Continued dollar weakness keeping export‑oriented earnings robust.

Positioning notes

  • Maintain exposure to high‑growth tech while scaling into defensive utilities if volatility spikes.
  • Short‑duration bond exposure remains attractive given the flat yield curve.
  • Diversify geographically; Asian equities still lag US performance, offering relative value.
