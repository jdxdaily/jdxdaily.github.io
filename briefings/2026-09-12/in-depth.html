TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,656.98, up +0.86%, as earnings beat expectations across technology and consumer discretionary, while the VIX fell to 15.84 (-11.21%), signaling reduced volatility risk. The 10‑year Treasury yield rose to 4.97% (+0.63%), reflecting lingering inflation concerns but a still‑benign Fed stance.

Sector breadth remains solid: all eleven major ETFs posted gains, led by XLK (+1.32%) and XLF (+0.67%). The market’s upside is now tied to forward‑looking data – especially the upcoming CPI release and the Fed’s September policy meeting.

Our editorial view: maintain a core long bias on quality growth names, but trim exposure to rate‑sensitive utilities and consumer staples ahead of potential tightening. Keep a modest hedge in short‑dated VIX calls to protect against a surprise volatility spike.
