US equities slip as yields rise, while tech rebounds on meta surge

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The broad market edged lower on Friday, with the S&P 500 down 0.02% and the Dow slipping 0.31%. The decline was driven by a fresh uptick in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 5.16%, pressuring rate‑sensitive sectors.

Despite the overall weakness, the NASDAQ 100 posted a modest gain +0.03%, anchored by a +4.5% jump in META. The rally highlights a divergence between growth‑oriented names and the lagging financials and industrials.
