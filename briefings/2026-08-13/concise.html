TL;DR

The US market saw a mixed session, with the Nasdaq 100 leading gains at +0.74% and the Dow Jones slipping -0.04%. The S&P 500 closed at 7,748.50, up +0.26%. Tech stocks, particularly NVDA, drove the rally, with the semiconductor company surging +3.03%.

Today's focus will be on the ongoing earnings season, with several key companies reporting. The macro calendar is relatively light, but investors will be watching for any signs of a shift in monetary policy.
