US equities slip as yields rise and volatility spikes

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

All major US equity indices closed lower on Thursday, with the S&P 500 down -0.48% to 7,619.98. The VIX surged +7.95% to 17.10, reflecting heightened risk appetite.

Yield pressure intensified as the 10‑year Treasury fell to 4.96% (-0.28%). Energy prices jumped, with WTI crude up +1.74% at 103.15, while defensive sectors such as health care and consumer staples posted the day’s only gains.
