TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,619.98, down -0.48%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 4.96% and the VIX surged +7.95%. Defensive sectors outperformed, while tech and financials lagged.

Rate‑sensitive stocks are under pressure after the Fed’s latest minutes hinted at a “higher‑for‑longer” stance. Commodity‑linked equities rallied on a +1.74% jump in WTI crude, but the broader market remains risk‑averse.

Our view: Expect continued intra‑day volatility. Look for short‑term buying opportunities in consumer‑discretionary and communication services, but keep defensive hedges in place.
