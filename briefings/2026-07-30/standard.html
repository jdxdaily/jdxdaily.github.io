US Markets Tumble as Rates and Oil Surge

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The US market sell-off deepened yesterday, with the S&P 500 falling -1.52% to 7,316.15 and the Nasdaq 100 dropping -2.06% to 27,192.31. Below the surface, sector rotation and macro trends are driving the action. The 10Y Yield surged +0.39% to 4.62, while WTI Crude jumped +5.54% to 83.65. These moves are having a ripple effect across the market.

One key observation is the underperformance of growth stocks, with NVDA falling -3.55% and TSLA dropping -2.97%. This is consistent with a rising rate environment, where value stocks tend to outperform. The Dow Jones fell -2.19% to 51,594.14, while the Russell 2000 declined -1.61% to 2,906.31.
