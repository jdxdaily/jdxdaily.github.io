TL;DR & Editorial Take

The US market sell-off accelerated yesterday, with the SPY down -1.52% and the QQQ falling -2.06%. The VIX spiked 13.45% to 20.66, while the 10Y yield jumped 0.39% to 4.62. Oil prices also surged, with WTI up 5.54% to 83.65.

The market's downward momentum is being driven by a combination of factors, including rising interest rates, higher oil prices, and a strong US dollar. The DXY fell -0.44% to 100.93, but remains near its recent highs.

Key takeaways:

  • The market's technical picture is deteriorating, with the SPY and QQQ both trading below their 50-day moving averages.
  • Sector internals are also weakening, with the XLF and XLK both down -1.60% and -2.64%, respectively.
  • The options market is pricing in higher volatility, with the VIX term structure steepening and the SPY options market showing a significant increase in put buying.
