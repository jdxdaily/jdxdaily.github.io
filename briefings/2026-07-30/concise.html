US Markets Tumble as Rates and Oil Prices Surge

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

The US market experienced a significant downturn in the previous session, with the S&P 500 falling by -1.52% to 7,316.15 and the Nasdaq 100 dropping by -2.06% to 27,192.31. This decline was largely driven by surging rates and oil prices, with the 10Y yield increasing by +0.39% to 4.62 and WTI crude rising by +5.54% to 83.65.

Today's session is expected to be heavily influenced by the ongoing earnings season, with several key companies set to report their quarterly results. Investors will be closely watching these reports for any signs of weakness or strength in the market, as well as any potential catalysts for future growth.
