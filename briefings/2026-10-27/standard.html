US equities rally on tech earnings, while volatility eases

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,676.77, up +0.70%, driven by a +1.09% jump in the Nasdaq 100 as earnings from NVDA and MSFT beat expectations. The VIX fell -5.45%, indicating reduced risk appetite after the Fed’s dovish tone.

Behind the headline, the market is pricing a slower‑roll‑off in tech growth and a modest easing of inflation pressures, reflected in the 10‑year yield slipping to 4.99. However, the under‑performance of consumer‑discretionary and communications sectors signals lingering concerns over spending and ad revenue.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Unexpected rise in US inflation data (CPI > 3.2%) reigniting rate‑hike fears.
  • Geopolitical escalation in the Middle East that spikes oil above 110 USD.
  • Weakening of AI demand leading to a -3% pullback in NVDA and related chips.
BULLISH TRIGGERS

  • Quarterly earnings beat from AMD or INTC reinforcing AI tailwinds.
  • Eurozone CPI surprise low, boosting risk appetite and dollar weakness.
