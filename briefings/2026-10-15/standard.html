US equities tumble as tech leads broad sell‑off, VIX spikes

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

All major US indices closed lower on October 14, with the Nasdaq 100 down ‑2.02% and the S&P 500 off ‑1.11%. The sell‑off was driven by a sharp correction in mega‑cap tech, led by NVDA and META, while defensive sectors such as health care and consumer staples posted double‑digit gains.

Behind the headline numbers, the VIX surged +10.48% to 16.87, reflecting heightened volatility expectations. The 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.19%, suggesting the market is pricing in a near‑term pause in rate hikes despite the Fed’s hawkish stance.
