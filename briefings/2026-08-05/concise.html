US Stocks Surge as Tech Leads the Charge

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

The US stock market rallied strongly, with the Nasdaq 100 leading the charge, up +3.32% to 29,733.16. The S&P 500 and Dow Jones also posted significant gains, rising +1.79% to 7,736.52 and +1.71% to 54,085.88, respectively.

Tech stocks were the main drivers of the rally, with NVDA surging +2.56% to 211.94 and AVGO jumping +6.61% to 418.16. The VIX rose +4.04% to 16.50, indicating increased market volatility.
