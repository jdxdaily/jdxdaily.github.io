TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,801.77, down ‑0.22%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield climbed to 5.28% and WTI crude surged past 90 USD. Defensive sectors lagged, while health‑care led the rally, reflecting heightened risk aversion.

Liquidity remains tight after the Fed’s last policy meeting; the market is pricing in a 30‑bp cut only after the November 2026 meeting, but the odds of a June cut have risen to 15 %.

Investors should watch for a break of the 7,850 S&P 500 level and the 5.30% 10‑year barrier. A sustained move above those thresholds could re‑ignite risk‑on flow, while a breach of the 7,750 support could trigger further downside.

  • Yield curve steepening pushes growth stocks lower; monitor NVDA and META for volatility.
  • Oil‑driven inflation risk lifts the VIX to 15.08, supporting defensive positioning.
  • Health‑care ETF XLV outperformed (+1.03%); consider selective exposure.
