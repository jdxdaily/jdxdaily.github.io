US equities slip as yields rise and oil spikes

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

All major US equity indices closed lower on Tuesday, with the S&P 500 down 0.22% to 7,801.77 and the Dow Jones off 0.66% at 51,179.87. Higher Treasury yields and a near‑2% jump in WTI crude pressured risk assets.

Defensive health‑care ETF XLV posted the only sector gain, up +1.03%, while the VIX rose modestly to 15.08, indicating lingering volatility.
