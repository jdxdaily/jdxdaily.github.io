US equities slip as yields rise, oil fuels sector pressure

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The major indices closed lower, with the S&P 500 at 7,801.77 (-0.22%) and the Dow Jones down -0.66%. Higher 10‑year yields (5.28%) and a jump in WTI crude to 90.77 drove risk‑off sentiment, while the VIX edged higher to 15.08.

Behind the headline, technology stocks showed resilience (e.g., AAPL +0.91%), but defensive sectors lagged, and the health‑care ETF (XLV) was the only sector posting a gain. Investors should watch the Fed’s upcoming policy guidance and the impact of oil‑price volatility on energy and industrials.

Spotlight: oil price surge and its market ripple

WTI crude jumped +2.82% to 90.77, the steepest one‑day gain since July. The move was sparked by a reported disruption at a Saudi export terminal and a weaker dollar (DXY +0.05%). Energy stocks fell despite the price rise, reflecting profit‑taking and concerns over demand.
