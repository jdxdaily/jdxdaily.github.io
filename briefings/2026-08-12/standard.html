US Markets End Mixed as Energy and Industrials Lead Gains

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

The US market ended the previous session with mixed results, as the S&P 500 and Dow Jones declined by -0.32% and -0.34% respectively, while the Nasdaq 100 fell by -0.33%. However, the Russell 2000 bucked the trend, rising by +0.32%. Below the surface, sector rotation was a key theme, with energy and industrials leading the gains.

A closer look at the sector ETFs reveals that XLE and XLI were among the top performers, rising by +1.25% and +0.60% respectively. This move was driven by the increase in WTI Crude prices, which rose by +2.36% to 84.07. Meanwhile, the VIX fell by -1.16% to 15.28, indicating a decrease in market volatility.
