TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,722.72, up +0.73%, driven by a broad tech bounce and a modest retreat in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 5.28%.

Liquidity remains ample: the VIX fell ‑6.59% to 15.31, while the DXY slipped ‑0.17% to 101.93. Risk‑on sentiment is reinforced by a +0.57% rise in Bitcoin, suggesting continued appetite for non‑core assets.

However, the market faces two near‑term headwinds: a potential Fed rate‑cut pause after the upcoming November meeting and lingering supply‑chain constraints in semiconductor production that could temper NVDA’s momentum.

  • Tech sector leads with +1.01% on XLK; semiconductor exposure remains a double‑edged sword.
  • Yield curve flattening: 10Y at 5.28% versus 2Y at 4.92% (data not shown), tightening financing conditions.
  • Commodities retreat: WTI at 91.11 USD, down ‑1.90%, limiting inflation upside.
