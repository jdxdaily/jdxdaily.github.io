US equities rally on tech earnings, yields ease

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 closed at 7,722.72, up +0.73%, driven by a broad tech bounce after strong earnings from NVDA and AVGO. The Nasdaq 100 outperformed, gaining +1.00%, while the VIX fell to 15.31, the lowest level since early 2024, signalling reduced near‑term volatility.

Behind the headline numbers, the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.28, a ‑0.76% move that supports risk assets. However, the modest rise in the DXY and a still‑elevated VIX tail suggest lingering macro uncertainty, especially around the Fed’s next policy decision.

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Unexpected Fed rate hike or hawkish language.
