TL;DR & Editorial Take

The major indices closed lower on Friday, with the S&P 500 down ‑0.58% and the Dow slipping ‑1.18%. Higher Treasury yields and a firmer dollar pressured growth stocks, while the energy sector found support from a 1 % rise in WTI crude.

Technical pressure is building on the SPY and QQQ as they test key 200‑day moving averages. The market breadth remains thin; only 6 of 11 sector ETFs posted gains.

We remain cautious ahead of the Fed’s September meeting. Expect volatility to stay elevated, with the VIX at 15.72 (+2.75%).

  • Yield curve steepening: 10‑year at 4.81% (+0.46%) lifts defensive positioning.
  • Energy outperforms: XLE up +1.11% on higher crude.
  • Tech correction deepens: NVDA down ‑2.01%, XLK up only +0.32%.
