US Markets End Mixed as Energy and Financials Lead Gains

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The US market ended the session mixed, with the S&P 500 down -0.06% and the Nasdaq 100 down -0.34%. Energy and financials led the gains, with XLE up +4.66% and XLF up +0.36%. The VIX rose +3.76% to 15.46, indicating increased volatility.

Below the surface, the market is showing signs of rotation, with investors moving out of growth stocks and into value stocks. The Russell 2000 was down -0.56%, indicating a shift away from small-cap stocks. The 10Y Yield rose +0.84% to 4.70, which could lead to a decrease in stock prices.
