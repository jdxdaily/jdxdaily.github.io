US equities tumble as tech leads broad sell‑off, volatility spikes

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 slipped -1.21% to 7,663.43, driven by a sharp retreat in mega‑cap tech stocks. The Nasdaq 100 fell -2.90%, pushing the VIX up +14.09% to 19.03, signalling heightened risk aversion.

Behind the headline, earnings pressure on NVDA and META, a firmer 10‑year Treasury yield at 4.95%, and a stronger dollar (101.69) are compressing margins for growth‑oriented firms. Defensive sectors (XLP, XLV, XLU) posted double‑digit gains, suggesting a short‑term rotation toward income and safety.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Further upside in the VIX above 22, indicating deepening risk aversion.
  • Fed signals a rate hike in the next meeting.

BULLISH TRIGGERS

  • Crude breaching $105, lifting energy ETFs.
  • Dollar index slipping below 101, easing pressure on equities.
