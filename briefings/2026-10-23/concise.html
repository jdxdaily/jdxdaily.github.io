A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

TL;DR

The S&P 500 fell -1.21% to 7,663.43, dragging the Nasdaq 100 down -2.90% to 28,958.69. Defensive sectors rallied, with the consumer staples ETF XLP up +3.31% and utilities XLU up +2.07%.
