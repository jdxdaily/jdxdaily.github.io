TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 slipped ‑1.21% to 7,663.43, dragging the Nasdaq 100 down ‑2.90%. Rate‑sensitive sectors lagged while defensive and real‑estate ETFs posted modest gains. The VIX surged +14.09% to 19.03, underscoring heightened risk appetite.

Key drivers were a worse‑than‑expected earnings beat from META (‑6.98%) and a sharp pull‑back in AI‑heavy names NVDA (‑4.40%) and AMZN (‑4.57%). The 10‑year Treasury yield fell to 4.95%, reflecting renewed demand for safe‑haven assets.

Our view: the market is entering a short‑term correction phase. Expect continued upside bias in defensive sectors and a potential rebound in AI stocks if earnings guidance improves.

  • Broad equity sell‑off driven by tech earnings disappointment.
  • Defensive ETFs (XLP, XLV, XLU) outperformed, signaling risk‑off rotation.
  • VIX spike and falling yields suggest near‑term volatility will remain elevated.
