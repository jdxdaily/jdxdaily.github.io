US equities tumble as rates rise, tech leads decline

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 slipped -1.09% to 7,659.44, driven by a broad tech sell‑off and a jump in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 5.10%. The VIX surged +21.45% to 19.99, underscoring heightened volatility ahead of the Fed’s policy outlook.

Energy rallied on lower crude, while defensive sectors (financials, utilities, consumer staples) posted modest gains, suggesting investors are rotating into safety as rate‑sensitive growth names falter.
