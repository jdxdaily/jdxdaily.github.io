US equities tumble as yields rise, VIX spikes

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

The major US indices closed sharply lower on Tuesday, with the S&P 500 down -1.09% to 7,659.44 and the Nasdaq 100 off -2.50% at 29,187.55. The market sell‑off was driven by a jump in the 10‑year Treasury yield to 5.10% and a 21.45% surge in the VIX to 19.99, underscoring heightened risk aversion.

Energy led the rally, with the XLE ETF up +2.52%, while technology lagged sharply; NVDA fell -1.43% and MSFT slipped -1.93%. Investors will watch upcoming earnings from AAPL and JPM for clues on earnings momentum.
