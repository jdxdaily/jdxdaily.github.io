TL;DR & Editorial Take

The US stock market experienced a mixed session, with the Dow Jones surging +1.03% and the Nasdaq falling -0.98%. The S&P 500 rose +0.21%, while the Russell 2000 gained +0.20%. The VIX decreased -2.46% to 18.21.

The 10Y yield fell -0.80% to 4.60, while WTI crude oil rose +4.26% to 82.64. Gold fell -0.36% to 4,021.60, and the DXY decreased -0.06% to 101.32.

Key takeaways include:

  • The Dow Jones' surge was led by gains in JPM and MSFT.
  • The Nasdaq's decline was driven by losses in NVDA and AMZN.
  • The VIX's decrease suggests a decrease in market volatility.
