US equities rally on strong earnings and easing inflation data

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The major indices closed higher, led by the Dow (+0.83%) and the S&P 500 (+0.59%), as earnings beat expectations and the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.24 %.

Behind the headline gains, the technology sector showed mixed signals: NVDA dipped -0.52% while MSFT surged +2.38%. The VIX fell to 14.84, indicating reduced volatility risk.
