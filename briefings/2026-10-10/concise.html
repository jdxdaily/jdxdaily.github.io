US equities rally on earnings beat, yields inch higher

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

All three major indices closed higher on Oct 9, with the S&P 500 up +0.59% at 7,811.54 and the Dow gaining +0.83% to 51,654.95. The rally was led by strong earnings from AMZN and a rebound in technology, while the 10‑year Treasury yield rose to 5.24%.

Volatility eased, with the VIX down ‑3.70% to 14.84. Commodity prices rose modestly; WTI crude hit 91.85 and gold surged +1.43% to 4,216.30.
