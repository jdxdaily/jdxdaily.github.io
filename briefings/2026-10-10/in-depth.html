TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,811.54, up +0.59%, driven by a strong tech earnings beat and a modest easing of inflation expectations. The 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 5.24% (+0.25 bp), keeping the Fed’s policy outlook ambiguous. Breadth remains solid, with 12 of 11 sector ETFs posting gains; only communications lagged.

While the rally appears robust, the market is vulnerable to a surprise rate‑cut signal or a sharp correction in the VIX, which fell ‑3.70% to 14.84. Investors should watch the upcoming Fed minutes for clues on the “higher‑for‑longer” stance.

Our stance: maintain a core long bias in high‑quality growth, but trim exposure to cyclical names ahead of potential macro‑driven volatility.

  • Broad market up; tech earnings beat fuels momentum.
