US stocks slip as yields rise, while meta spikes on ad revenue beat

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The broad market retreated, with the S&P 500 down -0.17% and the Dow off -0.26%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield climbed to 5.26%, pressuring rate‑sensitive sectors. Defensive utilities led the rally, gaining +1.17%, while technology lagged.

Meta Platforms surged +3.24% after beating ad‑revenue forecasts, offsetting weakness in Apple and Nvidia. The market’s near‑term direction hinges on upcoming CPI data and the Fed’s next policy signal.
