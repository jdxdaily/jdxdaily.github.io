TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,670.84, down -0.17%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield rose to 5.26% (+0.29%). Higher rates pressured consumer‑discretionary and financials, while the Nasdaq 100 eked out a +0.21% gain on strong earnings from META and AVGO. Market breadth remains thin; only 4 of 11 sector ETFs posted positive daily returns.

Our view: the market is entering a short‑term consolidation phase. Yield‑sensitive stocks face headwinds, but AI‑driven chipmakers and cloud leaders retain upside potential if earnings beat expectations. Investors should tilt toward defensive health‑care and utilities while keeping a watchful eye on the 10‑year yield’s trajectory.

Key takeaways:

  • Yield pressure persists – 10‑year at 5.26%, supporting a stronger dollar and weighing on high‑beta names.
  • Tech resilience – META up +3.24%, AVGO up +1.58% drive Nasdaq outperformance.
  • Sector breadth thin – only XLI, XLU, XLC, XLY posted gains; defensive sectors lag.
