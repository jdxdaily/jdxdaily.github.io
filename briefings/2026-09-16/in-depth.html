US equities slip as yields rise, oil rebounds and tech volatility spikes

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 closed at 7,585.73, down -0.45%, as the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped to 5.00% (+0.71%) and the VIX edged higher to 17.20. Energy led gains with XLE up +2.17% on a rebound in WTI crude, while technology lagged, dragging XLK -0.29%. The market’s downside bias is reinforced by higher rates, a firming dollar, and mixed earnings momentum.

Our view: the near‑term outlook remains bearish on equities until the 10‑year yield stabilises below 4.9% and the VIX retreats below 16.5. Defensive sectors (utilities, consumer staples) offer relative resilience, but the upside upside is limited to short‑term technical rebounds in the Nasdaq‑100.

Investors should watch for three catalysts: (1) the Fed’s September policy statement, (2) the upcoming CPI release (Oct 13), and (3) the earnings season for mega‑caps, especially NVDA and META.

  • Yield pressure: 10‑year at 5.00% (+0.71%) – risk‑off trigger.
  • Energy bounce: XLE +2.17% on WTI 104.78 – sector tailwind.
  • Tech weakness: XLK -0.29% and Nasdaq‑100 -0.65% – momentum drag.
