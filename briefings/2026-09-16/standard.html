US equities slip as yields rise and energy gains offset tech weakness

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The major indices closed lower, with the S&P 500 down -0.45% and the Nasdaq 100 off -0.65%. Higher Treasury yields and a firmer dollar pressured growth stocks, while energy ETFs rallied on a rebound in crude.

Behind the headline, the market is digesting a mixed macro backdrop: the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped to 5.00 % (+0.71 %), pushing valuation multiples lower, while the VIX rose modestly, indicating lingering volatility. The only bright spot was the NVDA bounce, but it was insufficient to offset sector‑wide weakness.
