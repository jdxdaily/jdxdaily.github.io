US equities rally on strong earnings and easing inflation expectations

The full picture: macro, technicals, options, analyst moves, sector internals, international context, and a full week ahead.

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 surged +0.86% to 7,656.98, driven by a cluster of earnings beats and a dip in the VIX to 15.84. 10‑year Treasury yields slipped to 4.97%, reinforcing the narrative of a softening inflation backdrop. Technology led the charge, with the Nasdaq 100 up +0.91%, while defensive sectors lagged.

Macro data points to a decelerating CPI trend and a modest rebound in commodity prices, notably gold at 4,408.90 (+1.02%). The dollar index (DXY) held near 99.12, limiting upside for export‑heavy equities. Crypto markets remain muted; BTC trades around 77,275 (+0.13%).

Our stance: maintain a bias toward growth‑oriented, earnings‑driven positions while trimming exposure to rate‑sensitive utilities and consumer staples. Watch for any surprise from the Fed’s September meeting minutes and the upcoming CPI release.

  • Broad market breadth remains positive: 8 of 11 sector ETFs posted gains.
  • Technical support holds for SPY at 7,600; resistance at 7,720.
  • Watchlist highlights: NVDA, META, AVGO for upside; XLV and XLU for downside risk.
