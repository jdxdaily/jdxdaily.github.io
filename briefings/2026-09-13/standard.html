US equities rally on strong earnings and easing inflation fears

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The major indices posted double‑digit gains on the back of robust Q3 earnings and a sharp pull‑back in the VIX, signaling renewed risk appetite. While the S&P 500 surged +0.86% to 7,656.98, the Nasdaq 100 outperformed with a +0.91% rise to 29,368.44. The rally was anchored by tech and consumer discretionary earnings that beat consensus, offsetting modest weakness in defensive sectors.

Behind the headline numbers, two dynamics merit attention: (1) the 10‑year Treasury yield slipped to 4.97% after the Fed’s dovish tone, narrowing the spread to equities; (2) the VIX’s ‑11.21% plunge to 15.84 suggests volatility is receding, but the market remains vulnerable to any surprise in upcoming CPI data.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Unexpected rise in CPI above 0.3% MoM could reignite rate‑hike expectations.
  • Escalation in Middle‑East conflict pushing oil above $110 bbl, spiking inflation.
  • Weak Q4 earnings from core tech names (NVDA, AMD) dragging the Nasdaq.
BULLISH TRIGGERS
  • Fed signals a longer pause or rate cuts, further lowering yields.
  • Positive surprise in U.S. retail sales indicating resilient consumer demand.
  • Strong AI‑related earnings across the board, reinforcing sector momentum.

Positioning notes

  • Risk‑on bias evident: equity exposure up, volatility down, yields falling.
  • Tech and consumer discretionary lead the rally; defensive sectors lag.
  • Liquidity remains ample; watch for a pivot if CPI surprises to the upside.
