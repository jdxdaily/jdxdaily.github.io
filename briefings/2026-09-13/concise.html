US equities rally on strong earnings and easing inflation fears

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

All three major indices closed higher on Tuesday, with the S&P 500 up +0.86% to 7,656.98 and the Nasdaq 100 gaining +0.91% to 29,368.44. The rally was anchored by robust earnings from technology and consumer discretionary firms and a retreat in the VIX to 15.84, the lowest level in three weeks.

Yield pressures eased as the 10‑year Treasury slipped to 4.97%, while commodity prices fell, with WTI crude down -2.37% at 100.05. Momentum remains positive but a resurgence in inflation data could test the upside.
