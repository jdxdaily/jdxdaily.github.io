US equities edge higher as tech leads, but rate outlook stays cautious

TL;DR & Editorial Take

The S&P 500 nudged up +0.17% to 7,650.50 while the Nasdaq 100 surged +0.67% on strong earnings from NVDA and AVGO. 10‑year Treasury yields climbed to 5.00%, keeping the risk‑off tone alive and the VIX down ‑4.08% at 14.81. Macro data point to a still‑tight monetary stance, limiting upside for rate‑sensitive sectors.

Our view: maintain a bias toward growth‑oriented, earnings‑driven names while trimming exposure to energy, utilities and materials that remain under pressure from lower commodity prices. Watch the 10‑year yield at 5.00% – a breach of 5.10% could trigger a broader sell‑off.

Key take‑aways:

  • Tech and semiconductor leaders outperformed; NVDA +1.34%, AVGO +2.97%.
  • Rate‑sensitive sectors (XLF, XLU, XLB) lagged, reflecting higher yields and a firming dollar.
  • Liquidity remains thin; 0‑DTE option flow shows a net put tilt of ‑12% on the SPY.
