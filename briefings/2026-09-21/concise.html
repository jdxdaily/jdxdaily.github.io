US equities edge higher as tech rebounds, yields climb

A 5-minute read of yesterday's US session and today's catalysts — what to watch before pre-market opens at 21:30 HKT.

2-3 page read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 nudged up +0.17% to 7,650.50, driven by a +0.82% jump in the XLK tech ETF and a solid +1.34% gain in NVDA. The Dow slipped -0.18% as industrials lagged, while the Nasdaq 100 outperformed with +0.67%.

Bond yields rose, the 10‑year Treasury hitting 5.00% (+1.03%). Energy prices fell sharply, with WTI crude down -5.83% to 94.45. Volatility eased, VIX at 14.81 (-4.08%).
