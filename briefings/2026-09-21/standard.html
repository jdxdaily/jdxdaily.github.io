US equities edge higher as tech leads, yields climb

A wider read of yesterday's session — sector rotation, pre-market action, watchlist scan, and what to watch into the US open at 21:30 HKT.

10-minute read · Topics: Macro · Geopolitics · Earnings · Sectors · Watchlist Download PDF

TL;DR

The S&P 500 nudged up +0.17% to 7,650.50, driven by a solid tech rally anchored by NVDA (+1.34%) and AVGO (+2.97%). Meanwhile, the 10‑year Treasury yield jumped +1.03% to 5.00%, reflecting renewed expectations of a tighter monetary stance.

Behind the headline, the market is wrestling with three undercurrents: (1) a flattening yield curve that hints at a possible recession signal, (2) a widening VIX dip to 14.81 suggesting complacency, and (3) a modest pullback in consumer‑discretionary ETFs (XLY, XLC) that could foreshadow a rotation toward defensive sectors.

What could break the tape

BEARISH TRIGGERS
  • Unexpected acceleration in CPI above 0.3% MoM could prompt a surprise rate hike.
  • Continued weakness in the Eurozone manufacturing PMI (-0.6%) spilling into US export demand.
