TL;DR & Editorial Take

The US stock market extended its gains on Friday, with the S&P 500 rising +0.70% to close at 7,489.72. The Nasdaq 100 outperformed, gaining +0.60% to 28,274.20. The Dow Jones also rose, increasing +0.53% to 52,485.03.

The market's gains were led by the tech sector, with NVDA and MSFT rising +2.93% and +3.02%, respectively. The VIX plummeted -6.44% to 15.99, indicating a decrease in market volatility.

Key takeaways from the session include:

  • The S&P 500 is approaching its 50-day moving average, which could provide resistance.
  • The VIX is trading near its lower end, which could indicate a potential bounce in volatility.
  • The tech sector is leading the market's gains, with NVDA and MSFT outperforming.
