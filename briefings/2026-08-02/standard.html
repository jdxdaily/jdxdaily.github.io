TL;DR

The US stock market ended the previous session on a positive note, with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 rising by +0.70% and +0.60% respectively. The gains were largely driven by the technology sector, with NVDA and MSFT leading the charge. However, beneath the surface, there are concerns about the Russell 2000 which fell by -0.50%, indicating potential weakness in the broader market.

Looking ahead, investors will be closely watching the upcoming earnings season, with several major companies set to report their quarterly results. The market will also be keeping an eye on the 10Y Yield, which rose by +1.76% to 4.74, and the VIX, which fell by -6.44% to 15.99. These indicators will provide valuable insights into the market's risk appetite and expectations for future growth.
